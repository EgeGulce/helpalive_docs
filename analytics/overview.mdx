The HelpAlive dashboard shows what your users do inside your product. It's not a general analytics platform — we focus on adoption and friction. The goal is to answer "what should we improve?" rather than "what happened?"

If you already run FullStory, Mixpanel, or Amplitude, HelpAlive sits alongside them: they remain your source of truth for analytics; we're where you go to take action.

## What's tracked at the event level

The SDK auto-captures a small set of high-signal events. From those, the dashboard rolls up:

| Surface | Source events |
|---------|---------------|
| **Users** | identify, pageview, click |
| **Sessions** | session_start, all events between |
| **Pages** | pageview |
| **Elements** | click, exposure |
| **Friction** | rage_click, dom_error, form_start without submit |

Aggregated dashboards refresh hourly. Per-user views show live activity, so admins always see what's happening right now.

## Tenant-level views

For B2B products, the unit of measurement is usually a tenant (workspace, account, organization), not an individual user. The dashboard surfaces:

- **Audience list** — all tenants ranked by activity, with a prioritization signal (volume × engagement).
- **Tenant detail** — sessions, users, pages, integrations health, recent activity.
- **Per-tenant usage** — user counts, session counts, event volume.

Tenants come from the `tenantId` you pass to `identify()`. If your app doesn't have multi-tenancy, all activity rolls under `default`.

## End-user profiles

Drill into any user and see:

- A timeline of their recent activity.
- Which platforms they use (browser, OS, mobile / desktop).
- Their plan, role, and account age.
- Privacy status (consent state, suppression flags).
- Recent chatbot conversations.

See [User profiles](/analytics/user-profiles) for the full surface.

## Coverage gaps

The most valuable view for product teams. Every user question that goes through the chatbot is classified:

- `no_retrieval` — nothing in the knowledge base matched.
- `weak_retrieval` — matches were thin or off-topic.
- `negative_rating` — user thumbed down despite a confident answer.

These are grouped into topics and ranked by **volume × gap score**. The result is a working inbox of "things to write next" or "features to fix" — see [Coverage gaps](/analytics/coverage-gaps).

## What we don't do

- **Session replay.** No video, no DOM snapshots.
- **Funnel building.** Adoption metrics come from autocaptured signals, not from manually defined funnels.
- **Cohort analysis tooling.** We surface adoption directly; if you want to slice by arbitrary cohorts, your dedicated analytics tool is the right place.
- **Data export to your warehouse.** Webhooks for chat events and DSAR exports exist; bulk warehouse syndication is not on the roadmap.
