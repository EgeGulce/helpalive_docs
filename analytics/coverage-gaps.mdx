The coverage-gaps inbox is one of the most actionable surfaces in HelpAlive. Every user question is classified on the way out of the chatbot, clustered into topics, and ranked so you know what to fix next.

If you've ever wished for a list of "things our docs are missing, ordered by impact", this is it.

## How questions get classified

When the chatbot answers a question, the retrieval pipeline emits a `CoverageStats` verdict:

| Class | When it fires |
|-------|---------------|
| `no_retrieval` | Nothing in the knowledge base matched the question above the relevance floor. |
| `weak_retrieval` | Matches were thin — top scores below threshold or off-topic semantically. |
| `negative_rating` | The user thumbed down the answer, regardless of retrieval confidence. |
| `covered` | Retrieval was confident and the user didn't thumb down. (Shown for completeness; not actionable.) |

A single question can carry multiple classifications. For example, weak retrieval that the user thumbed down counts twice in the gap score.

## Topic clustering

Individual questions are noisy ("how do I cancel?", "is there a way to stop my subscription?", "delete account please"). The dashboard clusters questions with **HDBSCAN** on their embeddings to surface topics:

- **"Cancellation flow"** — 47 questions in the last 30 days.
- **"Pricing for additional seats"** — 22 questions.
- **"How to invite a teammate"** — 18 questions.

Topics are stable across re-clusterings — adding a few new questions doesn't reshuffle the inbox.

## Ranking

Topics are ranked by `volume × gap score`:

- **Volume** — how many questions hit this topic in the selected window.
- **Gap score** — a weighted sum of `no_retrieval`, `weak_retrieval`, and `negative_rating` rates within the topic.

A topic asked 200 times with mostly confident answers ranks below a topic asked 50 times that the assistant consistently fails on.

## Working the inbox

**Dashboard → AI → Coverage gaps**

For each topic, you see:

- **Question count** in the selected window.
- **Severity badge** (`high`, `medium`, `low`) based on gap score.
- **Sample questions** from the cluster.
- **Sample answers** the assistant gave (so you can spot hallucinations).
- **Recommended action** — typically "add a doc" or "improve a doc".

Click into a topic to see all questions, all answers, and rating distribution.

## Resolving a topic

There are three ways to resolve a coverage gap:

1. **Write or update a doc** — add the missing knowledge to your source URL or upload a new file. Re-crawl and publish.
2. **Add a custom response** — for things that aren't doc-shaped (pricing, internal policies, plan-specific responses).
3. **Mark as not actionable** — for questions that are out of scope (e.g. "what's the weather?").

After publishing, watch the next 24–48 hours of traffic. The gap score should drop sharply if the new content was a good fit. If not, the topic stays in the inbox at a lower rank.

## Filters

| Filter | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| **Time range** | Compare last 7 vs last 30 days to see what's getting worse / better. |
| **Source** | Filter to questions from a specific page or tenant. |
| **Class** | Show only `no_retrieval` (clear gaps) or only `negative_rating` (quality issues). |
| **Plan / role** | Are pro users hitting different gaps than free users? |

## What it isn't

- It's not a CSAT survey. The thumbs up/down feeds in but isn't the whole signal.
- It's not a chatbot QA dashboard. Quality issues surface here, but the goal is product action — write docs, fix flows — not LLM tuning.
- It's not a feature-request log. Some topics will be feature requests; treat those as input to product, not as docs work.

## Privacy

Coverage gaps surface the **text** of user questions because the whole point is to read them and respond. This is the only place in HelpAlive analytics where verbatim user input is shown. Question text:

- Lives in the `chatbot_messages` store, not in the event analytics tables.
- Is redacted for emails, phone numbers, and other PII patterns before storage.
- Is scoped per customer — your team only sees questions from your own users.

Users can request deletion via DSAR ([Privacy → DSAR](/privacy/dsar)) and their question history is removed from the inbox.
