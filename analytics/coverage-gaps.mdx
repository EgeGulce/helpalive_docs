The coverage-gaps inbox is one of the most useful surfaces in HelpAlive. Every question the chatbot couldn't answer well is captured, grouped into topics, and ranked — so your team always knows what to fix next.

## How questions get flagged

Whenever the chatbot answers, we watch for three signals:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Nothing matched** | We searched your knowledge base and didn't find anything relevant. Usually means your docs are missing something. |
| **Weak match** | We found something, but it wasn't a strong fit for the question. |
| **User thumbs-down** | The user rated the answer poorly, even when we were confident. |

A question can carry more than one signal — for example, a thumbs-down on a weak match counts twice toward the gap score.

## How topics are built

Individual questions are noisy ("how do I cancel?", "is there a way to stop my subscription?", "delete account please"). HelpAlive groups similar questions together into **topics** so you see patterns instead of single questions:

- **Cancellation flow** — 47 questions in the last 30 days
- **Pricing for additional seats** — 22 questions
- **How to invite a teammate** — 18 questions

Topics stay stable as new questions come in — adding a few new ones doesn't reshuffle the inbox.

## How they're ranked

Topics are ranked by **volume × gap score** — how often it comes up, weighted by how badly the chatbot is failing on it. A topic asked 200 times with mostly confident answers ranks below a topic asked 50 times that the chatbot can't handle.

## Working the inbox

Open **AI → Coverage gaps** in the dashboard. Each topic shows:

- **Question count** in the selected window.
- **Severity** — `high`, `medium`, or `low`.
- **Sample questions** from the cluster.
- **Sample answers** the chatbot gave (so you can spot weak ones).
- **Recommended action** — usually "add a doc" or "improve a doc".

Click a topic to see all questions, all answers, and rating distribution.

## Resolving a gap

There are three options:

1. **Add or update content** in your knowledge base. Re-index, then publish.
2. **Add a custom answer** for things that aren't doc-shaped — internal policies, plan-specific responses, time-sensitive notices.
3. **Mark it as not actionable** for off-topic questions ("what's the weather?").

After publishing a fix, watch the next 24–48 hours of traffic. The gap score should drop sharply if the new content was the right fit. If not, the topic stays in the inbox at a lower rank.

## Filters

| Filter | Use case |
|--------|----------|
| **Time range** | Compare the last 7 days to the last 30 to see what's getting worse or better. |
| **Source** | Narrow to questions from a specific page or workspace. |
| **Signal** | Show only "nothing matched" (clear gaps) or only thumbs-downs (quality issues). |
| **Plan / role** | Find out if pro users hit different gaps than free users. |

## What it isn't

- **Not a CSAT survey.** The thumbs up / down feeds in but isn't the whole picture.
- **Not chatbot-tuning telemetry.** Quality issues surface here, but the goal is product action — write docs, fix flows — not LLM tuning.
- **Not a feature-request log.** Some topics will be feature requests; treat those as input to product, not as docs work.

## Privacy

The inbox shows **the actual text** of user questions because reading them is the whole point. To keep this safe:

- Question text lives in a separate, secure store from your event analytics.
- Personal information (emails, phone numbers, IDs) is automatically redacted before storage.
- You only see questions from your own users — never from other HelpAlive customers.

If a user requests deletion, their question history is removed from the inbox. See [Data deletion](/privacy/dsar).
