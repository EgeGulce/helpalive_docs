The HelpAlive chatbot is a context-aware product assistant that lives inside your app. Once you train it on your documentation, it can answer your users' questions in real time — already aware of which page they're on, who they are, and what they were just doing.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where it runs** | Inside your product, in a dedicated chat panel |
| **Isolation** | Won't conflict with your styles or scripts |
| **Response speed** | Streams the answer word-by-word as it's generated |
| **Knowledge sources** | Your docs site (crawled), uploaded files (PDF, DOCX, Markdown), or pasted text |
| **Setup time** | Around 10 minutes |
| **Workflow** | Edit in draft → preview → publish |

## What makes it different

A generic AI chatbot reads the message and replies. The HelpAlive chatbot does more: every answer is grounded in **four kinds of context**, in real time.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Page context" icon="window">
    The exact page the user is on, the title, and what's visible on screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="User identity" icon="user">
    Who the user is, their role, plan, and which workspace they belong to — straight from your `identify()` call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent activity" icon="clock">
    What they clicked, where they got stuck, and which errors they saw in the last few minutes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your knowledge base" icon="book-open">
    Your docs, help articles, and custom guidance — searched for relevance before every answer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The result: instead of "How can I help?", the chatbot opens with something like *"I see you were trying to add a teammate from the Billing page. Want me to walk you through it?"*

## What your users see

When you turn the chatbot on, your users see a launcher button in the corner of your product. When they open it, they get:

- A personalized greeting based on who they are and what page they're on.
- Three suggested questions for the current page.
- A real-time chat with thumbs-up / thumbs-down feedback.
- The option to start a fresh conversation any time.

End users don't install anything. The chatbot appears automatically once HelpAlive is integrated.

## Privacy

Chat conversations live in their own secure store and **never** mix with your adoption analytics. The conversation text is treated separately from event data, with its own redaction and retention rules. See [Privacy overview](/privacy/overview) for the full picture.
