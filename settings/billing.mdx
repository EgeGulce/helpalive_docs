The **Billing** tab in **Settings** is where you subscribe, manage payment, and see what your plan includes.

Open your dashboard and go to **Settings → Billing**.

## Plan

The **Plan** card says where your subscription stands and has one button.

| What the card says | The button | What it opens |
|--------------------|------------|---------------|
| **Not subscribed yet.** | **Subscribe** | Stripe Checkout, to pay for your plan. |
| **Subscribed.** | **Manage billing** | Stripe's billing portal: your card, your invoices and receipts, and cancelling. |

After you pay, the card shows your plan within a few seconds. If you close Checkout without paying, nothing is charged.

When your plan needs a subscription, the card says **Your agent turns on for your users once you subscribe**, and the agent stays off for them until you do. You can still install HelpAlive and train the agent first.

The card also tells you when something needs your attention:

- **Your last payment did not go through.** Stripe tries again; open **Manage billing** to update your card.
- **Subscribed until a date, then it ends.** You cancelled, and the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for.
- **Your subscription has ended.** Press **Subscribe** to start again.

Below the button, the card lists what your plan includes each month: a number of guides and a number of chat messages. What you have used this month, how far past your plan the agent may go, and extra packs are on [Agent → Configuration](/agent/configuration#usage-and-limits).

## Team seats

The **User Limits** card shows how many active teammates you have against your plan's seat limit, with a bar.

| Threshold | What the card says |
|-----------|---------|
| **80% or more used** | Approaching user limit. |
| **100% used** | User limit reached. You can't invite or reactivate anyone until a seat opens up. |

To free a seat, deactivate someone in [Team](/settings/team).
