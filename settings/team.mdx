The **Team** tab in **Settings** is where you manage who has access to your HelpAlive dashboard — your teammates, not your end users.

Open your dashboard and go to **Settings → Team**.

## Three sections, one screen

| Section | What it's for |
|---------|---------------|
| **Team Members** | Everyone currently active on your dashboard. |
| **Invites** | Invitations you've sent that haven't been used yet. |
| **Inactive Members** | Teammates who've been deactivated and don't have access right now. |

## Inviting a teammate

Click **Invite Teammates**. Add one or more email addresses, then send all the invites at once. The dialog shows how many spots your plan has left.

Each address must be in a domain your organization allows (see [Security & Access](/settings/security)).

HelpAlive emails each invitee a link to set their password and join. The link lasts 3 days. Invites appear in the **Invites** section with a status:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Pending** | Waiting for the invitee to accept. You can resend it or revoke it. |
| **Used** | They joined. They're now in **Team Members**. |
| **Expired** | The link timed out. Resend it to send a fresh link for another 3 days. |

**Clear Old Invites** removes the used and expired ones from the list.

Each teammate can send up to 50 invite emails a day, resends included.

## Everyone is an admin

Everyone on your team has full access to the dashboard: they can invite teammates, train and publish the agent, change settings and billing, and delete data. There are no member roles to assign.

## Deactivating a teammate

In **Team Members**, press the deactivate button on their row. It revokes their access without deleting them, and they move to **Inactive Members**. You can't deactivate yourself.

Deactivation is reversible. Press **Reactivate** in the **Inactive Members** section to give a former teammate access back, subject to your plan's seat limit.

## Seat limits

Your plan caps the number of active teammates. If you've hit the limit, **Reactivate** and **Invite Teammates** are blocked until you deactivate someone else or change your plan. See [Billing](/settings/billing) for the current count.
