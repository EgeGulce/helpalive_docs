**Settings → Setup & API Key** is the one place to set HelpAlive up. New workspaces land on it after their first login.

## The steps

Three steps to go live, plus one optional step we recommend for security. Each turns green by itself once HelpAlive sees it working.

| Step | Who | What it covers |
|------|-----|----------------|
| **1. Add HelpAlive to your app** | Your developer, about 10 minutes | The tag and the `identify()` call, with an AI coding agent prompt, the code, or Google Tag Manager. See [Installation](/installation). |
| **2. Teach the agent your product** | Anyone on your team | Your sources on **Agent → Train**, and whether each is live for the agent. See [Train](/agent/train). |
| **3. Turn it on and try it** | Anyone on your team | Whether the agent is on, with a link to the rollout settings on **Agent → Configuration**, where you switch it on and choose who sees it. |
| **4. Verify your users** | Optional, recommended · your developer, about 15 minutes | Your signing secret, the code that makes a user token, and **Only allow verified users**. See [Verify your users](/sdk/verify-users). |

## Is it working?

The panel beside the steps has one line per step, each with a check once it works. Press the refresh button to check again after trying your app. [Installation → Check that it works](/installation#check-that-it-works) says how each line decides.

## Your project key

The key your tag uses, shown under the panel. It is public: it is already in the code the setup page gives you.

- **Create your project key** if your workspace doesn't have one yet.
- **Regenerate** if you want a new one. The old key may still be accepted for up to 30 seconds, so update your tag straight away. You can regenerate once an hour.
