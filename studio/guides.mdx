**Guides** are how the agent helps with a task in your product, not just answers about it. When a user asks for help with something they want to do, the agent makes a guide on the spot, for that task on the page they're on, and walks through it on your real product. By the end, the task is done, not just read about.

There is nothing to author. The agent works from what the user asked, the page in front of them, and the docs you trained it on.

## What your users see

- The agent offers the guide in the chat, and the user starts it.
- Each step highlights the real element on the page: a button, a field, a tab.
- In **Does the steps** mode, the agent fills in the form and presses the buttons for them. In **Shows how** mode, it points at each field and waits while they do it.
- The user can stop at any time.

## Choosing what guides do

On [Agent → Configuration](/agent/configuration), **What it does** sets the mode for everyone who gets the agent, and anyone in the rollout list can have their own. The rollout there also decides who gets the agent, guides included.

## How guides stay accurate as your product ships

The agent finds elements by their **meaning** (role, label, place on the page) as the page is right now, not by stored selectors. A button labelled "Save" stays findable even if its CSS class changes, and a guide made today follows today's page.

## Usage

Each guide started counts toward your plan's guides for the month. See [Plans](/plans/overview).
