## What's in every plan

These are guarantees, not upsells:

- **Two-layer PII redaction** (browser + server-side Presidio).
- **Per-customer data isolation** with optional database-level RLS.
- **DSAR-ready privacy tooling** — async deletion jobs with audit trail.
- **Consent modes** — auto and required, plus runtime toggle.
- **Data subject controls** — suppression, deletion, export on request.

## Usage metering

Plans are metered by **events per month** rather than seats. Dashboard users (your team) are typically unlimited or generously capped depending on plan.

The SDK auto-captures behavioral events; for cost predictability we'll work with you in the pilot to size the right plan based on your traffic. Burst handling and over-quota behavior are configurable per customer.
