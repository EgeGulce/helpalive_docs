A HelpAlive plan is a monthly subscription. It includes a number of guides and chat messages each month, and a number of teammates on your dashboard.

## How HelpAlive is priced

- **Guides and chat messages.** Your plan includes a number of each per month. Tracking your users' activity isn't metered.
- **When the month's allowance is used**, the agent stops by default until the month starts again. You can let it go a set percentage further, billed at the end of the month, on [Agent → Configuration](/agent/configuration), or buy extra packs.
- **Extra packs.** Once you're subscribed, you can buy a pack of guides or of chat messages on [Agent → Configuration](/agent/configuration). It's added straight away and used after your plan's monthly amount. What you don't use carries over to the next month, and the month after, until it's used, as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel, unused extras end with your subscription, at the end of your current billing month.
- **Teammates.** Your plan caps how many active teammates your dashboard has.

You subscribe, and manage your card and invoices, on [Settings → Billing](/settings/billing), which shows the price before you subscribe.

## What's in every plan

These are guarantees, not upsells:

- **Two-layer PII redaction** — once in your users' browsers, once on our servers.
- **Per-customer data isolation** with optional defense-in-depth controls.
- **DSAR-ready privacy tooling** — async deletion jobs with audit trail.
- **Consent modes** — auto and required, plus runtime toggle.
- **Data subject controls** — suppression, deletion, export on request.
