The model below is how plans are shaped. Today, the right path is to [talk to us](https://helpalive.com/contact) — we'll size a pilot that fits your team's traffic.

## How HelpAlive is priced

Three components, designed so what you pay grows with the value you get:

1. **Per end-user session** captured by the SDK. This scales with your traffic, not with your team size — a 5-person team running a high-traffic SaaS pays more than a 50-person team with low traffic.
2. **AI usage** for chatbot answers, generated guidance, and (in development) automatically generated guide drafts. Token-equivalent metering, billed monthly.
3. **Per resolution** (in development). Once we have real outcome data — guides completed, support questions deflected, friction events resolved — we'll layer in a per-resolution charge so customers pay more only when more real friction is being closed.

Compare to:
- **Seat-based pricing** (Pendo, Intercom): penalizes team growth even when you're not using the product more.
- **Pure session-based** (segment-style): pays the same whether the tool actually helps anyone or not.

Our model is built so revenue and customer outcomes move together.

## What's in every plan

These are guarantees, not upsells:

- **Two-layer PII redaction** — once in your users' browsers, once on our servers.
- **Per-customer data isolation** with optional defense-in-depth controls.
- **DSAR-ready privacy tooling** — async deletion jobs with audit trail.
- **Consent modes** — auto and required, plus runtime toggle.
- **Data subject controls** — suppression, deletion, export on request.
