A HelpAlive plan is a monthly subscription. It includes a number of guides and chat messages each month, and a number of teammates on your dashboard.

## How HelpAlive is priced

- **Guides and chat messages.** Your plan includes a number of each per month. Tracking your users' activity isn't metered.
- **Teammates.** Your plan caps how many active teammates your dashboard has.

To subscribe or change plans, [talk to us](https://helpalive.com/#early-access).

## What's in every plan

These are guarantees, not upsells:

- **Two-layer PII redaction** — once in your users' browsers, once on our servers.
- **Per-customer data isolation** with optional defense-in-depth controls.
- **DSAR-ready privacy tooling** — async deletion jobs with audit trail.
- **Consent modes** — auto and required, plus runtime toggle.
- **Data subject controls** — suppression, deletion, export on request.
