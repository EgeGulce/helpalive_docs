If your question isn't answered below, [get in touch](https://helpalive.com/#early-access).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What counts toward usage?">
    Guides started and chat messages. Your plan includes a number of each per month, and **Agent → Configuration** shows how many you've used. Tracking your users' activity isn't metered, and your end users are never counted as seats.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a team member?">
    Team members are people on **your team** with access to the HelpAlive dashboard — usually product, support, and engineering. Your end users (the people using your product) are never team members and don't count toward any seat cap.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I change plans later?">
    Yes, [talk to us](https://helpalive.com/#early-access). Your card, invoices and cancelling are under **Manage billing** on **Settings → Billing**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens when my users use up the month's guides or chat messages?">
    By default the agent stops until the month starts again: no new guides are offered, and new chat messages get "I can't answer right now." You can let it go a set percentage further, billed at the end of the month, or buy extra packs, both on **Agent → Configuration**. It warns you at 80%, when the plan's amount is used, and when it stops.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I buy extra guides or chat messages?">
    Yes, once you're subscribed. On **Agent → Configuration**, under **Buy extra**, buy a pack of guides or of chat messages. It's added straight away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to extras I don't use?">
    They carry over to the next month, and the month after, until you use them, as long as your subscription continues. Your plan's monthly amount is always used first, so extras only go down once it runs out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my extras if I cancel?">
    Your subscription and any unused extras end together, at the end of your current billing month. Until then everything keeps working as normal.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does HelpAlive work alongside tools like Intercom or Zendesk?">
    Yes. HelpAlive's AI chatbot is a guidance layer inside your product — it doesn't replace your support stack. Native integrations with help-desk tools are on the roadmap; today, you can route chatbot escalations or coverage-gap alerts via webhooks (talk to us about the webhook spec).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are supported?">
    Card payments through Stripe, from **Settings → Billing**. For anything else, [talk to us](https://helpalive.com/#early-access).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a long-term commitment?">
    No. Your plan renews each month until you cancel under **Manage billing**, and it runs to the end of the period you paid for.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you support self-hosted or on-prem?">
    Self-hosted is on the Enterprise roadmap. It's not the near-term focus, but if it's a hard requirement for your organization, talk to us.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does HelpAlive handle data deletion and DSAR?">
    Every plan ships with DSAR-ready privacy tooling. Async deletion jobs with audit trails, user and tenant suppression, and consent controls are standard. See [DSAR deletion](/privacy/dsar) for the workflow.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
