---

If you have questions about how HelpAlive plans work, what counts toward your limits, or how billing is handled, you'll find answers below. If your question isn't covered here, [contact the team](https://helpalive.com/contact) and someone will get back to you.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I change plans later?">
    Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    HelpAlive is currently in early access. [Contact the team](https://helpalive.com/contact) to request access and discuss trial options.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a team member?">
    Team members are users with access to the HelpAlive dashboard — typically the people on your product, support, or engineering team who configure and monitor HelpAlive. End users navigating your product are not counted toward your team member limit.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does HelpAlive work with my existing support tools?">
    Yes. HelpAlive works alongside tools like Intercom and Zendesk. Integrations — including live chat handoff, CRM sync, Slack alerts, and webhooks — are available on Premium and Enterprise plans. See the [integrations overview](/integrations/overview) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will HelpAlive replace my current support solution?">
    Not today — but that's where we're heading. HelpAlive works alongside tools like Intercom or Zendesk, catching problems before they become tickets. The goal is a helpful colleague, not a chatbot ambush.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Credit card payments are accepted for Pro and Premium plans. Enterprise customers can arrange invoiced billing. [Contact the team](https://helpalive.com/contact) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a contract or minimum commitment?">
    Pro and Premium plans are month-to-month with no long-term contract required. Enterprise plans may include annual contracts with volume discounts available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
