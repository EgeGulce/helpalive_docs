---
title: "HelpAlive integrations: live chat, CRM, Slack, and webhooks"
description: "Connect HelpAlive to your live chat tool, CRM, Slack workspace, or any custom endpoint to route friction signals to the right team and act fast."
---

HelpAlive integrates with the tools your support and customer success teams already use. When a user struggles, you can escalate to a live agent with full session context, flag the user in your CRM, alert a Slack channel, or forward events to any endpoint via webhooks. All integrations are configured in the HelpAlive dashboard under **Settings → Integrations**.

<Note>
  All integrations are currently in development and will be available soon. To express interest or get notified when an integration launches, [contact us at helpalive.com/contact](https://helpalive.com/contact).
</Note>
