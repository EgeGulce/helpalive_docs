---
title: "Sync struggling users to Salesforce or HubSpot CRM"
description: "Flag users experiencing persistent friction in Salesforce or HubSpot so your customer success team can reach out before those users churn."
---

When HelpAlive detects that a user is repeatedly hitting friction — failing to complete key flows even after guidance — it can create or update a record in your CRM. Your customer success team sees the flag, reaches out proactively, and helps the user before the frustration turns into churn. No ticket required, no user complaint needed.

<Note>
  The CRM sync integration is coming soon. To express interest or get notified when it launches, [contact us at helpalive.com/contact](https://helpalive.com/contact).
</Note>

## Use case

A user signs up, starts onboarding, and gets stuck on the same step three days in a row. HelpAlive's guidance attempts don't fully resolve the issue. HelpAlive updates the user's record in Salesforce or HubSpot with a friction flag and a summary of the session. A CS rep sees the flag in their daily queue and sends a personal outreach email before the user ever thinks about cancelling.

## Supported CRMs

- **Salesforce** — creates or updates a Contact or Lead record
- **HubSpot** — creates or updates a Contact record

## How to configure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integrations settings">
    In the HelpAlive dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → CRM**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your CRM">
    Choose **Salesforce** or **HubSpot** and follow the OAuth prompts to authenticate. HelpAlive requests read/write access to Contact and Lead records only.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map your user identifier">
    Tell HelpAlive which CRM field corresponds to the user ID you pass to `HelpAlive.identify()`. This is how HelpAlive matches a session to an existing CRM record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure friction thresholds">
    Set the conditions that trigger a CRM sync — for example, three or more friction events within a seven-day window, or a walkthrough dismissed without completion on a critical flow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Save your configuration. Use the **Send test record** button to verify that the friction flag appears correctly in your CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What data is synced

HelpAlive writes the following fields to the CRM record when a sync is triggered:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| User ID | The identifier passed to `HelpAlive.identify()` |
| Friction event count | Total number of friction events recorded for this user |
| Last friction timestamp | ISO 8601 timestamp of the most recent friction event |
| Session summary | A plain-language description of where the user struggled and what guidance was attempted |

All synced fields are written to a dedicated HelpAlive property group so they don't overwrite any existing CRM data.
