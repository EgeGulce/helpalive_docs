title: "Get Slack alerts when users hit friction in your product"

---

HelpAlive can notify a Slack channel when friction patterns reach a threshold that warrants human attention — a user has hit a wall repeatedly, a walkthrough hasn't resolved the issue, or a live chat escalation has been triggered. Instead of waiting for a support ticket or a churn event, your team sees what's happening in real time and can act immediately.

<Note>
  The Slack alerts integration is coming soon. To express interest or get notified when it launches, [contact us at helpalive.com/contact](https://helpalive.com/contact).
</Note>

<Tip>
  Create a dedicated **#helpalive-alerts** channel in your Slack workspace. Keeping HelpAlive notifications in a single channel makes it easy to triage, avoids noise in general support channels, and lets you set up Slack workflow automations on top of the alerts.
</Tip>

## Alert types

HelpAlive sends Slack alerts for the following events:

- **Friction detected** — a user deviated from the expected path and HelpAlive recorded a deviation event
- **Walkthrough not resolving** — a user has been shown guidance two or more times on the same flow without completing it
- **Escalation triggered** — a user was escalated to live chat or flagged for support follow-up

## How to configure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integrations settings">
    In the HelpAlive dashboard, go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your Slack workspace">
    Click **Connect workspace** and complete the Slack OAuth flow. HelpAlive requests permission to post messages to channels you select.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a channel">
    Choose the Slack channel where HelpAlive should send alerts. We recommend creating a dedicated **#helpalive-alerts** channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your alert types">
    Enable or disable individual alert types — friction detected, walkthrough not resolving, and escalation triggered — based on your team's needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and test">
    Save your configuration. Click **Send test alert** to verify that the message appears correctly in your selected channel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example alert message

When HelpAlive sends a Slack alert, it looks like this:

```text
HelpAlive Alert — Escalation triggered

User: user_123
Route: /onboarding/step-3
Time: 2026-05-08 10:30 UTC

The user was escalated to live chat after hitting friction on /onboarding/step-3
three times in this session. Deviation score: 0.87.

Session ID: sess_abc
```

Each alert includes the user ID, the route where friction occurred, the timestamp, a plain-language summary of what happened, and the session ID so you can look up the full session in the HelpAlive dashboard.
