This page is for the developer who adds HelpAlive to your app, and for the admin who plans the work. It covers what you add, what to decide first, and how you know you're done.

## Prerequisites

- A HelpAlive account, with your project key from **Settings → Setup & API Key**.
- Access to the layout every page of your app shares.
- About 10 minutes for the install, and about 15 more to verify users.

## What you add

HelpAlive runs in your users' browsers, inside your app. You add three pieces of code, and nothing on your server unless you verify users:

| Piece | Where | What it does |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| One script tag | The `<head>` of every page | Loads HelpAlive. It carries your project key, which is public. |
| `identify()` | Wherever your app knows the signed-in user | Tells HelpAlive who is signed in. The agent appears only after it runs. |
| `reset()` | Your logout code | Forgets the user, so the next person on that browser starts fresh. |

There is nothing to tag in your pages, and no API to connect. The agent reads your pages as they are. [Make your app easy for the agent](/sdk/agent-ready) has a few tips that help.

Verifying users adds one more piece: a few lines on your server that sign each user id. Your signing secret stays on your server.

## Decide before you install

These three choices are hard to change later, because they shape the data HelpAlive keeps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Which id identifies a user">
    Use your app's own user id: one that never changes and is unique across your app. Don't use an email address if users can change theirs. A changed id starts a new user, with no chat history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whether you have tenants">
    A tenant is one of your customers: the company, workspace or account a user belongs to. If your app has them, pass the tenant's id as `tenantId`. **Controls** uses tenants to decide who gets the agent and to set limits, and **Reports** and **Audience** group users by them. If your app has no such thing, leave `tenantId` out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Whether to verify users">
    Without verification, HelpAlive trusts the user id your page sends, so someone who edits the page could pose as another user. With it, your server signs each id. It is optional and recommended before you open the agent to all users. See [Verify users](/sdk/verify-users).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Done means

The install is done when the four required checks on **Settings → Setup & API Key** are green, and the panel says **Everything is working.** The four checks are:

1. **HelpAlive is on your pages**
2. **It knows who is signed in**
3. **The agent has your docs**
4. **The agent answers in your app**

The last two need your admins as well: docs published on **Train**, and the agent switched on in **Controls**. **Users are verified** is the fifth, optional check.

## Troubleshooting

The install fails quietly when a piece is missing: no error appears on your page, and the agent doesn't show. [Troubleshooting](/sdk/troubleshooting) lists each silent failure and how to spot it.
