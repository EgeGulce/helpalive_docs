The **Agent → Configuration** page decides whether the agent is on, who sees it, what it does for them, and how much they can use.

Open your dashboard and go to **Agent → Configuration**. If the agent has no settings yet, press **Set up the agent** first.

<Note>
  Changes here are a draft until you press **Save changes** in the bar at the bottom of the page. **Discard** puts back what was saved. They don't go through the draft and publish flow of Train, Personalization and Appearance. A page of your app that was already open picks up a saved change on its next page load.
</Note>

## Rollout

| Row | What it decides |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Agent** | The switch. Off, nobody sees the agent and your analytics keep running. |
| **Who gets it** | **Everyone** who signs in to your app, **A percentage** of them, or **Selected** tenants and people only. |
| **What it does** | **Does the steps**: it fills in the form and presses the buttons for them. **Shows how**: it points at each field and waits while they do it. |

With **A percentage**, the same people get it each time, so raising the number only adds more. Tenants and people you add to the list always have it.

With **Selected**, add tenants and people from the list of those your app has sent through `identify()`, or type the `tenantId` or `userId` your app passes. Anyone in the list can have their own **What it does**.

## Usage and limits

| Row | What it shows |
|-----|---------------|
| **Guides this month** | Guides started against what your plan includes, and the day it starts again. |
| **Chat messages** | Chat messages against what your plan includes. |
| **How far over your package** | How much past your plan the agent may go this month. |
| **Buy extra** | Packs of guides or chat messages, once you're subscribed. |
| **Limits** | The most one person, one tenant or your whole organisation can use. |

Warnings appear at the top of this section when you reach 80% of a month's guides or chat messages, when you have used what your plan includes, and when the agent has stopped.

### Past your plan

Set **How far over your package** from 0% up to the most your plan allows. At 0%, the agent stops at your plan: once it is used, no new guides are offered and new chat messages get "I can't answer right now." until the month starts again, and nothing extra is billed. Above 0%, it keeps working up to that much more, billed at the end of the month, and the row shows the most that can cost.

### Buy extra

Once you're subscribed, each pack adds guides or chat messages on top of what your plan includes. Pressing one opens Stripe Checkout. Once you've paid, the pack shows here in a few seconds; with a payment method that takes longer to clear, such as a bank debit, it's added when the payment clears. If you close Checkout without paying, nothing is charged, and a Checkout page left open for more than 30 minutes expires, so press the pack again.

Your plan's monthly amount is used first, then your packs. What you don't use carries over to the next month, until it's used, as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel, unused extras end with your last billing month.

### Limits

A table of chat messages and guides started, for **Each person**, **Each tenant** and **Your organisation**, each over an hour or a day as the table shows. An empty box uses our default. When someone reaches a limit, the chat says "I can't answer right now. Try again in a little while."

## Danger zone

Both actions ask you to confirm first.

- **Reset training data** deletes every knowledge source and its indexed content. Your personalization and appearance stay. After a reset, the agent has nothing to answer from until you add sources in **Agent → Train**. You can't undo it.
- **Remove** switches the agent off and resets its configuration to the defaults. Your training data is kept.
