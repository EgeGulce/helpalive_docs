The **Agent** is HelpAlive's in-app AI assistant. It lives inside your product, knows the user's current page, role, and recent activity, and answers their questions grounded in your documentation — in real time.

You configure the Agent from the **Agent** section of your dashboard. There are five tabs:

| Tab | What you do here |
|-----|------------------|
| **Train** | Add your knowledge sources — docs URL crawls, PDFs, Word documents, or pasted text. |
| **Personalization** | Set the Agent's tone, length, and custom rules. Add specific answers for special-case questions. |
| **Test** | Run a batch of test questions against your draft changes before you publish. |
| **Appearance** | Match the Agent to your brand — colors, position, welcome message, layout. |
| **Configuration** | Visibility, rate limits, and danger-zone reset controls. |

## What makes the Agent context-aware

A generic AI chatbot reads the message and replies. The HelpAlive Agent does more: every answer is grounded in **four kinds of context**, in real time.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Page context" icon="window">
    The exact page the user is on, the title, and what's visible on screen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="User identity" icon="user">
    Who the user is, their role, plan, and which workspace they belong to — straight from your `identify()` call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Recent activity" icon="clock">
    What they clicked, where they got stuck, and which errors they saw in the last few minutes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your knowledge base" icon="book-open">
    Your docs, help articles, and custom answers — searched for relevance before every reply.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The result: instead of opening with "How can I help?", the Agent says something like *"I see you were trying to add a teammate from the Billing page — want me to walk you through it?"*

## What your end users see

When you turn the Agent on, your users see a launcher button in the corner of your product. When they open it, they get:

- A personalized greeting based on who they are and what page they're on.
- Three suggested questions for the current page.
- A real-time conversation, with thumbs-up / thumbs-down feedback.
- The option to start a fresh conversation any time.

End users don't install anything. The Agent appears automatically once HelpAlive is integrated.

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Where it runs** | Inside your product, in a dedicated chat panel |
| **Isolation** | Won't conflict with your styles or scripts |
| **Response speed** | Streams the answer word-by-word as it's generated |
| **Knowledge sources** | Your docs site (crawled), uploaded files (PDF, DOCX, Markdown), or pasted text |
| **Setup time** | Around 10 minutes |
| **Workflow** | Edit → preview on the **Test** tab → publish |

## Privacy

Conversations live in their own secure store and **never** mix with your behavioral analytics. Personal data in messages is automatically redacted before storage. See [Privacy](/privacy/overview) for the full picture.
