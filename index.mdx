HelpAlive is an AI agent that lives inside your web app. It answers your users' questions from your own docs, and it does tasks for them or shows them how, one step at a time. You add one script and one `identify()` call, it works on any web app, and it reads the page's structure instead of taking screenshots.

This site is for the people who set HelpAlive up: the admin who configures it in the dashboard and the developer who installs it.

## What your users get

- **Answers from your docs.** Users ask in their own words and get a short answer that fits the page they're on.
- **Tasks done for them, or shown to them.** When a user wants something done, the agent offers a guide. It does the steps itself, or highlights each control so the user clicks.
- **A clear hand-back.** Steps only the user can do, such as uploading a file, go back to them.

You decide which of these each tenant or person gets, on **Controls**.

## Start here

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [app.helpalive.com/signup](https://app.helpalive.com/signup). The [Quickstart](/quickstart) follows the same steps as **Settings → Setup & API Key**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the script and identify()">
    Your developer adds one tag to your pages and one `identify()` call for the signed-in person. See [Install the script](/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Teach it your product">
    On **Train**, add your help site or upload files, then publish them. See [Train](/dashboard/train).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it on">
    On **Controls**, turn the agent on and choose who gets it. See [Controls](/dashboard/controls).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check it works

Sign in to your own app as a user who should get the agent. A round button appears in the corner of the page. Open it and ask a question your docs answer.

If the button doesn't appear, see [Troubleshooting](/sdk/troubleshooting).
