These are the same steps as the setup page in your dashboard: **Settings → Setup & API Key**. The page fills in your project key for you and turns each step green by itself once it sees that step working.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add HelpAlive to your app">
    Your developer, about 10 minutes. Two pieces of code: a tag that loads HelpAlive on every page, and one call that tells it who is signed in. The agent only appears for signed-in people.

    Paste the tag into the `<head>` of every page. Copy it from the setup page, where your project key is already in it:

    ```html
    <!-- Paste before </head> -->
    <script src="https://cdn.helpalive.com/sdk/helpalive.js" data-api-key="YOUR_PROJECT_KEY" async></script>
    ```

    Then, wherever your app already knows the signed-in person, tell HelpAlive who they are:

    ```javascript
    // Lets the calls below run before our script has finished loading. Keep it first.
    window.HelpAlive = window.HelpAlive || { q: [] };
    ["identify", "reset", "setConsent", "track"].forEach((m) => {
      if (!HelpAlive[m]) HelpAlive[m] = (...args) => HelpAlive.q.push([m, args]);
    });

    // On every page while someone is logged in, starting right after login
    HelpAlive.identify({
      userId: user.id,              // your own id for this person
      tenantId: company.id,         // optional: their company or workspace; leave out if your app has none
    })

    // When they log out, and when their session times out.
    HelpAlive.reset()
    ```

    Only `userId` is required. [Installation](/installation) has the optional fields, where the tag goes in each framework, Google Tag Manager, and a prompt for your AI coding agent.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Teach the agent your product">
    Anyone on your team. Add your help center, docs site or files on **Agent → Train**, then publish them. The agent answers your users from them, and HelpAlive keeps them up to date. See [Train](/agent/train).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it on and try it">
    Anyone on your team. Switch the agent on and choose who sees it in the rollout settings, on **Agent → Configuration**. Then open your app in another tab, sign in, open the agent and ask it something.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your users (optional, recommended)">
    Your developer, about 15 minutes. HelpAlive works fully without this step. With it, your server signs each user id, and once you turn on **Only allow verified users**, nobody can pretend to be someone else. See [Verify your users](/sdk/verify-users).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check that it works

The setup page's **Is it working?** panel has one line per step. Press the refresh button after trying your app to check again. [Installation → Check that it works](/installation#check-that-it-works) says how each line decides.
