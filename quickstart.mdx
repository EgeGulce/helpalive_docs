This page takes you from a new account to the agent answering inside your app. It is for whoever sets HelpAlive up: an admin, with a developer for step 1.

The steps are the same four as **Settings → Setup & API Key** in your dashboard. That page fills in your project key for you, and each step turns green by itself once HelpAlive sees it working.

## Prerequisites

- A HelpAlive account. Sign up with your work email, enter the 6-digit code we email you, and fill in your profile and your organization. Then add a card to start your 7-day free trial; nothing is charged that day. See [Billing and usage](/dashboard/billing#free-trial).
- Access to your app's code, or to its Google Tag Manager container.
- Your help center, docs site or help files.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add HelpAlive to your app">
    Your developer, about 10 minutes. Two pieces of code: a tag that loads HelpAlive on every page, and one call that tells it who is signed in. The agent only appears for signed-in people.

    Paste the tag into the `<head>` of every page. **Settings → Setup & API Key** shows it with your project key already in it.

    ```html
    <!-- Paste before </head> -->
    <script src="https://cdn.helpalive.com/sdk/helpalive.js" data-api-key="YOUR_PROJECT_KEY" async></script>
    ```

    Then, wherever your app knows the signed-in person, tell HelpAlive who they are. Do this right after login and on every page load while they stay signed in.

    ```javascript
    // Lets the calls below run before our script has finished loading. Keep it first.
    window.HelpAlive = window.HelpAlive || { q: [] };
    ["identify", "reset", "setConsent", "track"].forEach((m) => {
      if (!HelpAlive[m]) HelpAlive[m] = (...args) => HelpAlive.q.push([m, args]);
    });

    // On every page while someone is logged in, starting right after login
    HelpAlive.identify({
      userId: user.id,              // your own id for this person
      tenantId: company.id,         // optional: their company or workspace; leave out if your app has none
    })
    ```

    Put this in your logout code, apart from the code above:

    ```javascript
    // In your logout code, and wherever your app ends a session.
    HelpAlive.reset()
    ```

    Only `userId` is required. [Install the script](/installation) covers where the tag goes in each framework, Google Tag Manager, TypeScript and the prompt for an AI coding agent. [Identify users](/sdk/identify) lists every `identify()` field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Teach the agent your product">
    Anyone on your team. Open **Train** and choose **Add a website** or **Upload a file**. When the crawl finishes, press **Publish** in the bar at the top of the dashboard. The agent answers your users only from what is published. See [Train](/dashboard/train) and [Publishing](/dashboard/publishing).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn it on and try it">
    Anyone on your team. Open **Controls**. If the page says the agent has no settings yet, press **Set up the agent**. Choose **Who gets it**, switch the agent **On**, press **Save changes**, then **Turn on**. See [Controls](/dashboard/controls).

    Open your app in another tab and sign in, or reload a page if it was already open. Then open the agent and ask it something.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Verify your users (optional, recommended)">
    Your developer, about 15 minutes. HelpAlive works fully without this step. Without it, HelpAlive trusts the user id your page sends. With it, your server signs each id, and once you turn on **Only allow verified users**, nobody can use the agent as someone else. See [Setup & API Key](/dashboard/setup) and [Verify users](/sdk/verify-users).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If someone already had your app open when you turned the agent on, their browser still remembers that it was off. They see the chat button within a minute or a few page loads. People who open your app later see it on their first page.
</Note>

## Check it works

At the top of **Settings → Setup & API Key**, **Is it working?** shows five checks:

- **HelpAlive is on your pages**
- **It knows who is signed in**
- **The agent has your docs**
- **The agent answers in your app**
- **Users are verified** (optional)

Try your app, then press the refresh button to check again. When the first four are green, the band says **Everything is working.**

## Troubleshooting

- **The step 3 card is greyed out.** The agent cannot turn on before a source is published. Publish one on **Train** first.
- **The tag is on the page but nothing appears.** The agent only shows after `identify()` runs for a signed-in person. Check that your code calls it on every page load, and that `userId` is not empty.
- **Nothing shows after you switched it on.** Wait a minute and load a few pages, as in the note above. Check that **Who gets it** includes you.
- **Controls says "Your agent is off for your users until you subscribe."** Subscribe on **Settings → Billing and usage**. See [Billing and usage](/dashboard/billing).
