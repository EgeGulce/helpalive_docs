**HelpAlive is your best product advisor — for every user, in every session.** It watches what users do inside your SaaS, learns what your *best* users do, and steps in when others go off track — with guided walkthroughs in the moment, and context-aware answers when they ask for help.

One product replaces an analytics tool, an in-app chatbot, and a guide builder. You stay the supervisor; HelpAlive runs the work.

## Why this exists

Your analytics tool sees what users do. Your support chatbot answers what users ask. Your guide builder runs what someone hand-coded. None of those see the user's *real-time* context — the page they're on, the click they just rage-tapped, the form they abandoned three minutes ago.

HelpAlive is built around that gap. The same SDK that captures behavior also feeds the AI assistant fresh page context at every turn, so the agent answers like someone who's actually watching — not someone reading a generic FAQ.
