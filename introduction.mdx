HelpAlive is a guidance layer for B2B SaaS. We help your users find value in your product through an in-app AI assistant, and we show your team where adoption is breaking down with insights drawn from real usage.

You install one `<script>` tag, call `identify()` after login, and HelpAlive does the rest — no manual event tagging, no schema design, no per-feature SDK calls sprinkled through your app.

## What HelpAlive does

HelpAlive is built around three shipped pieces.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Lightweight SDK" icon="zap">
    An ~11&nbsp;KB browser SDK that auto-captures clicks, pageviews, form submits, rage clicks, and DOM errors. One script tag, one `identify()` call.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI product assistant" icon="message-circle">
    A Preact widget rendered in a Shadow DOM, trained on your docs, aware of the user's current page, role, and recent activity. Answers stream over SSE.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Adoption analytics" icon="chart-line">
    Per-user, per-tenant, and per-page views of who's using what. Plus a coverage-gaps inbox that ranks the questions your assistant can't answer.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who it's for

HelpAlive is built for small-to-mid B2B SaaS teams (roughly 20–200 people) who care about adoption and retention but can't justify the cost or setup of FullStory or Pendo. Typically one product person plus a couple of engineers — no dedicated analyst required.

It's also a fit for mid-market teams (200–1,000) who already run a heavyweight analytics tool and want a guidance layer on top of it.

<Note>
  HelpAlive does not replace your analytics platform. We sit alongside it. Your existing tools keep capturing what happened — HelpAlive is the layer that helps your users find value and tells your team what to fix next.
</Note>

## What HelpAlive is not

- **Not session replay.** No video, no DOM snapshots.
- **Not a general analytics platform.** We surface adoption metrics to prove guidance works, not to be a dashboard destination.
- **Not a developer-facing product.** The customer is a product team, not an analyst writing SQL.
- **Not a data warehouse.** Events are stored for adoption analytics, not for export to your data lake.
