**HelpAlive is an AI agent for every user in your SaaS.** It watches what users do inside your product, learns what your *best* users do, and steps in when others go off track — with guided in-app walkthroughs in the moment, and context-aware answers when they ask for help.

**One product replaces an analytics tool, an in-app chatbot, and a guide builder.** You stay the supervisor; HelpAlive runs the work.

## What HelpAlive does

<CardGroup cols={3}>

## Why this exists

Your analytics tool sees what users do. Your support chatbot answers what users ask. Your guide builder runs what someone hand-coded. None of those see the user's *real-time* context — the page they're on, the click they just rage-tapped, the form they abandoned three minutes ago.

HelpAlive is built around that gap. The same SDK that captures behavior also feeds the Agent fresh page context at every turn, so the Agent answers like someone who's actually watching — not someone reading a generic FAQ.

## How the dashboard is organized

Your dashboard has three main sections, plus settings:

| Section | What you do |
|---------|-------------|
| **Agent** | Train the AI assistant on your docs, customize its tone, design, and behavior, then publish. |
| **Studio** | Work the Topics inbox of unanswered questions, manage the pages your Agent runs on, ship in-app guides. |
| **Insight** | Browse workspaces and users, see adoption reports, and monitor Agent sentiment. |
| **Settings** | Team, integration & API key, security, billing, privacy management. |
