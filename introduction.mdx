HelpAlive is an AI-powered in-product guidance platform that watches how your users navigate, learns what successful paths look like, and automatically steps in when someone goes off track. Instead of waiting for users to open a support ticket, HelpAlive detects friction in real time and delivers contextual walkthroughs right inside your product. This page explains what HelpAlive is, what problem it solves, and who it's built for.

## Why HelpAlive?

HelpAlive addresses four outcomes that every product and support team cares about.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Less churn" icon="trending-down">
    Users who get stuck and don't find help leave. HelpAlive intercepts those moments before they become cancellations.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fewer tickets" icon="ticket">
    Guided walkthroughs answer the question before the user reaches for the chat widget or emails support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Faster activation" icon="zap">
    New users reach their first value moment sooner when the product itself shows them where to go next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Upsell opportunities" icon="arrow-up-right">
    Session context tells you exactly when a user is ready to hear about a higher-tier feature — surface the message at the right moment.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How it fits in your stack

<Note>
HelpAlive sits alongside your existing tools rather than replacing them. It layers on top of your product UI with a single script tag and works with whatever frontend framework or server-rendered stack you already use. Your CRM, help desk, and chat tools keep working as before — HelpAlive enriches them with real-time session context through its integration layer.
</Note>
