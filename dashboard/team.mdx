**Team & access** is where you invite your team, choose who can join, and set how long a sign-in lasts. It is the first tab of **Settings**, at **Settings → Team & access**. **Settings** is the last item in the sidebar.

Everyone on your team is an admin: "Everyone on your team can change every setting."

## Invite your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    On **Your team**, press **Invite teammates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add email addresses">
    Type an address, such as `teammate@company.com`, and press **Add** or Enter. Add as many as you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press the send button, such as **Send 2 invites** (it counts the addresses you added), and confirm your password when asked. Each person gets an email with a link to join your workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If an email can't be sent, the toast says why and offers **Copy link**, so you can send the link yourself. See [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting).

### Manage invites

The **Invites** panel lists each invite with its **Status** (**Waiting**, **Joined** or **Expired**), when it **Expires**, and who sent it.

- An invite lasts 3 days. **Home** reminds you of one that ends soon.
- **Resend** sends it again with a fresh link and a new expiry date.
- **Revoke** cancels an invite that is still waiting. **Delete** removes a joined or expired one.
- **Clear joined and expired** tidies the list.

Your workspace can send a limited number of invite emails a day, new invites and resends together.

### Manage your team

**Your team** lists each person's **Name**, **Email**, **Job role** and **Last sign-in**.

- **Deactivate** opens **Remove team member?**. Press **Remove**: they lose access to the dashboard at once and stay in the list as deactivated. You can't deactivate yourself.
- Open the deactivated people under the list and press **Reactivate** to give someone access again.

## Who can join

**Who can join** lists **Your allowed domains**: "Only these domains can be invited or sign up."

- With no domains, anyone with a work email address can be invited.
- Type a domain under **Domain to allow**, such as `company.com`, and press **Add domain**.
- Wildcards such as `*` can't be added. Add each domain you want to allow.
- Removing a domain doesn't remove the people who already joined with it.

## How long a sign-in lasts

Our defaults sign your team out after 7 days without activity, and after 30 days at most, whichever comes first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Change it">
    On **How long a sign-in lasts**, press **Change**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the times">
    **Without activity**: from 15 minutes to 30 days. **At most**: from 1 hour to 90 days. The time without activity can't be longer than the most.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Save changes** and confirm your password. Anyone already past the new limits is signed out at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Use our defaults** puts it back to 7 days and 30 days.

## Check it works

Your invitee's row turns **Joined** once they finish signing up. They appear under **Your team**.

## Troubleshooting

- **An invite says Expired.** Press **Resend** for a fresh link.
- **"Your workspace has sent as many invite emails as one day allows."** Your workspace reached today's limit on invite emails, new invites and resends together. Try again tomorrow.
- **"Invite created for teammate@company.com, but email could not be sent."** The invite exists, but its email didn't go out. A resend says "Invite refreshed, but email could not be sent" instead. The toast adds why:
  - "This address isn't valid." **Revoke** that invite, check the address for typos, and invite the right one.
  - "Earlier emails to this address bounced or were refused." The address can't receive our emails. Press **Copy link** and send the link another way.
  - "The email service didn't answer. Try again in a minute." Wait a minute, then press **Resend**.
  - "Something on our side stopped it. We're on it." Press **Copy link** and send the link yourself, or try **Resend** later.
- **The invitee can't sign up from the link.** They must use the email address the invite was sent to. Their domain must be in **Your allowed domains**, if you set any.
- **The invite page says "This workspace is full."** A workspace can have at most 100 members. Deactivate someone you no longer need, or email support@helpalive.com.
- **You are asked for your password.** Some actions here ask you to confirm it. See [Your account](/dashboard/account#confirm-your-password).
