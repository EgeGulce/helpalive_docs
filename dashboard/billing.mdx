**Billing and usage** is where you subscribe, see what you have used this month, buy more, and get your invoices. It is the third tab of **Settings**, at **Settings → Billing and usage**. Payments go through Stripe.

Prices are shown on this page and in Stripe Checkout.

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Settings → Billing and usage**. The band says **Not subscribed yet.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Subscribe">
    Stripe Checkout opens on the page. It says what you get **Every month**, that it **Starts** today and renews each month, and how **Extra packs** work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Enter your card in Stripe's form. "Nothing is charged until you pay." If the form can't load, use **Pay on Stripe's page instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The page says "Subscribed to" your plan, "Your plan starts today." The band then shows **Subscribed. Renews on** the date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your plan needs a subscription before the agent can run, the band adds "Your agent turns on for your users once you subscribe."

## Your plan

The band at the top shows your plan, where your subscription stands, **This period**, with how far into it you are, and the **Price**. Once you subscribe it shows the **Next invoice** with the card that pays it, and **Payment method and invoices**.

**Payment method and invoices** opens Stripe's portal in a new tab. There you change your card, see invoices and cancel.

## Guides and Chat messages

One card for each, for this plan period:

- **Used**: how many your users have used.
- **In your plan**: what your plan includes.
- **Extra this month**: packs and anything carried over.
- **Past the package**: how many more you allow on **Controls** under **How far over your package**, or "stops at your package".

Under the bar, the card lists the daily limits ("The most: ...") and where each extra came from: **Carried over from last month**, **Pack bought** on a date, or **Added by HelpAlive**.

## Packs

A pack adds a fixed number of guides or chat messages to your plan.

- Packs can be bought only while you are subscribed.
- They are charged to the card on file.
- They are used only after your plan's own amount runs out.
- They carry over to the next month while you stay subscribed, and end with your subscription.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the kind">
    Press **Add guides** or **Add chat messages**. **What is on sale** lists the pack sizes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many">
    Pick how many packs. The dialog shows the **Amount** and the card it will charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press the button that names the pack and its price. The page says, for example, "Added 500 guides. They carry over."
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your bank wants to confirm the payment, the dialog says "Your bank wants to confirm this payment." Nothing is charged until it does.

## Next bill, payment method and invoices

- **Next bill** shows the amount and date, and any overage. Set to cancel, it shows **When it ends** instead.
- **Payment method** shows the card on file.
- **What you have paid** shows the last months and "A month, on average".
- **Invoices** lists each one with its **Date**, **Amount** and **Status** (**Paid**, **Not paid** or **Cancelled**), with a PDF to download. **Show more invoices** loads older ones.

## Cancel

Open **Payment method and invoices** and cancel in Stripe's portal. The band then says "Subscribed until" the end date, "then it ends." Unused packs end with the subscription; the page lists what is left. To keep them, turn cancellation off in the same place.

## A failed payment

- While Stripe retries, the band says "Your last payment did not go through. Stripe is trying again; update your card under Payment method and invoices."
- Once Stripe stops retrying, it says "Your last payment did not go through. Update your card under Payment method and invoices."
- Once Stripe stops retrying, anything past your plan's amount pauses until the payment goes through. Everything your plan includes keeps running. When this pauses something, we email your team and **Controls** says "Overages are paused until your last payment goes through."
- **Home** warns you when your card expires within 30 days, with **Update card**.

## Check it works

After you subscribe, the band says **Subscribed. Renews on** a date, and **Invoices** lists your first invoice.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Extra guides and messages are for subscribers."** Subscribe first, then buy packs.
- **"Add a card in Stripe first."** No card is on file. Add one under **Payment method and invoices**.
- **"Your card was declined. Update it in Stripe, then try again."** Nothing was charged. Update your card and retry.
- **"Billing is not set up yet. Contact us to change your plan."** Billing isn't available for your workspace yet. Write to support@helpalive.com.
- **"Checkout cancelled. Nothing was charged."** You closed Checkout. Press **Subscribe** again.
