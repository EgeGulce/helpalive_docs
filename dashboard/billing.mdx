**Billing and usage** is where you subscribe, see what you have used this month, buy more, and get your invoices. It is the third tab of **Settings**, at **Settings → Billing and usage**. Payments go through Stripe.

Prices are shown on this page, "plus tax where it applies", and in Stripe Checkout.

## Free trial

A new workspace starts with a 7-day free trial. You add a card as the last step of sign-up, and nothing is charged that day.

- The card step shows how many guides and chat messages the trial includes. Nothing goes past them during the trial: there is no overage.
- While the trial runs, the sidebar shows **Free trial** with the days left, such as "5 days left", and what happens on the last day. It opens this page.
- We email you three days before the trial ends.
- When it ends, your plan starts and your card pays for it. To pay nothing, cancel under **Manage billing** before the trial ends.
- Each workspace gets one trial.

If you leave sign-up at the card step, **Finish later** signs you out. The next time you sign in, the card step opens again: "Your workspace is ready. Add a card to start your free trial."

## Subscribe

If your workspace has no subscription, for example after one has ended, subscribe here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Go to **Settings → Billing and usage**. The band says **Not subscribed yet.**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Subscribe">
    Stripe Checkout opens on the page. It says what you get **Every month**, that it **Starts** today and renews each month, and how **Extra packs** work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Enter your card in Stripe's form. "Nothing is charged until you pay." If the form can't load, use **Pay on Stripe's page instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The page says "Subscribed to" your plan, "Your plan starts today." The band then shows **Subscribed. Renews on** the date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your plan needs a subscription before the agent can run, the band adds "Your agent turns on for your users once you subscribe."

## Your plan

The band at the top shows your plan, where your subscription stands, **This period**, with how far into it you are, and the **Price**, "plus tax where it applies". During the free trial the band names the plan **Free trial**, shows **Free trial** in place of **This period**, gives the price as free "during the trial", and says which plan starts on which day. The sentence under it says what the trial includes until it ends: "Extra packs are used only after the trial's amount runs out." Once you subscribe it shows the **Next invoice** with the card that pays it, and **Manage billing**.

**Manage billing** opens Stripe's portal in a new tab. There you change your card, see all your invoices and cancel.

## Guides and Chat messages

One card for each, for this plan period:

- **Used**: how many your users have used.
- **In your plan**: what your plan includes.
- **Extra this month**: packs and anything carried over.
- **Past the package**: how many more you allow on **Controls** under **How far over your package**, or "stops at your package".

During the free trial, **In your plan** reads **In your trial** ("for the whole trial") and **Extra this month** reads **Extra in the trial**.

The **Guides** card also says: "Every guide starts from a chat message, so guides need chat messages left too." While chat messages are used up, it warns instead: "No new guides can start while chat messages are used up: every guide starts from a chat message."

Under the bar, **Daily caps** explains: "The most that can be used in a day, so one busy person or tenant can't use up your plan. Past a cap, nothing more is served or billed until usage drops back under it." It shows **Your organisation**, **Each tenant** and **Each person**, each with its figure or **No cap**, and whether it is "the default" or "set by you". **Change caps on Controls** opens **Controls**. The card also lists where each extra came from: **Carried over from last month**, **Pack bought** on a date, or **Added by HelpAlive**.

## Packs

A pack adds a fixed number of guides or chat messages to your plan.

- Packs can be bought only while you are subscribed, the free trial included. A pack bought in the trial is charged to your card at once, and what is left of it carries into your first paid month.
- They are charged to the card on file. Each price shows "plus tax where it applies".
- They are used only after your plan's own amount runs out.
- They carry over to the next month while you stay subscribed, and end with your subscription.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the kind">
    Press **Add guides** or **Add chat messages**. **What is on sale** lists the pack sizes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how many">
    Pick how many packs. The dialog shows the **Amount** and the card it will charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press the button that names the pack and its price. The page says, for example, "Added 500 guides. They carry over."
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your bank wants to confirm the payment, the dialog says "Your bank wants to confirm this payment." Nothing is charged until it does.

If a pack's payment is refunded, or a dispute over it is lost, the pack's unused guides or chat messages are taken back. Ones already used stay used.

## Invoices and what you have paid

- **Invoices** lists your newest three, each with its **Date**, **Amount** and **Status**, and a PDF to download. **All invoices in Manage billing** opens the rest in Stripe's portal.
- **What you have paid** shows the last months and "A month, on average".

## Cancel

Open **Manage billing** and cancel in Stripe's portal. The band then says "Subscribed until" the end date, "then it ends." Unused packs end with the subscription; the page lists what is left. To keep them, turn cancellation off in the same place.

## A failed payment

When a renewal payment fails:

- **At once**, anything past your plan's amount pauses. Everything your plan includes keeps running. **Controls** says "Overages are paused until your last payment goes through."
- **After 14 days** of failing, or sooner if Stripe marks the payment unpaid, guides and chat stop and the agent disappears for your users. It comes back as soon as the payment goes through.
- **Every page** of the dashboard shows a bar: "Your last payment didn't go through", with the date to update your card by, and **Update card**. Once the agent has stopped, the bar says "Your assistant is paused", and **Home** says **Paused.**
- On this page, the band says "Your last payment did not go through. Update your card by" that date "under Manage billing to keep your assistant running."
- We email everyone on your team when the payment first fails, halfway through the 14 days, three days before the agent stops, and when it stops.

Update your card under **Manage billing**. **Home** also warns you when your card expires within 30 days, with **Update card**.

## Check it works

After you subscribe, the band says **Subscribed. Renews on** a date, and **Invoices** lists your first invoice.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Extra guides and messages are for subscribers."** Subscribe first, then buy packs.
- **"Add a card in Stripe first."** No card is on file. Add one under **Manage billing**.
- **"Your card was declined. Update it in Stripe, then try again."** Nothing was charged. Update your card and retry.
- **"Billing is not set up yet. Contact us to change your plan."** Billing isn't available for your workspace yet. Write to support@helpalive.com.
- **"Checkout cancelled. Nothing was charged."** You closed Checkout. Press **Subscribe** again.
