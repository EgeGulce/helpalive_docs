**Controls** is where you decide whether the agent is on, who gets it and what it does for them. It also sets how much of your plan they can use. It is the fifth item in the sidebar.

Nothing on Controls waits for **Publish**. When you press **Save changes**, it applies at once, and the toast offers **Undo**. Turning the agent on is the one exception: its box saves for you.

## Prerequisites

- At least one published source on **Train**. The switch can't turn the agent on without one. Until then the band says **Off: no docs published.** and, under the switch, "Turning it on needs your docs first. Add them on Train".
- HelpAlive installed in your app, with `identify()` running for signed-in people. See [Quickstart](/quickstart).

## Turn the agent on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the agent, the first time only">
    A new workspace shows "The agent has no settings yet." Press **Set up the agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who gets it">
    Under **Who gets it**, choose **Everyone**, **Selected** or **A percentage**. See [Who gets it](#who-gets-it).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what it does">
    Under **What it does**, choose **Does the steps** or **Shows how**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch it on">
    Move the switch in the band to **On**. The **Turn the agent on** box opens at once. It names who the agent turns on for, and any Setup step not finished. If no test has run yet, it first recommends testing, with a **Go to Test** link. Press **Turn on** to save; there is no **Save changes** after it. **Not yet** puts the switch back to off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off, move the switch to **Off** and press **Save changes**. Turning off asks nothing. While off, the band says "Off. Nobody sees it; your analytics keep running."

<Note>
After you turn the agent on, people who already have your app open don't see the chat button straight away. Their browser remembers the earlier setting, so the button appears within a minute or a few page loads. Turning it off, or changing who gets it, reaches them the same way. Someone who opens your app later sees the change on their first page.
</Note>

## The band

The dark band at the top says the agent's state in one line, such as "On for everyone." or "On for 25% of your tenants." It adds since when it has been on and what is left this month. While you have unsaved changes it shows **Not saved yet**.

While the agent is off, the band also recommends what to do first:

- "Setup is not finished: ..." with **Finish Setup**;
- "Only allow verified users is off. We recommend it, so nobody can use the agent as someone else." with **Set it up**;
- "Choose who gets it below before you turn it on."

While the agent is off, the band also has **Go to Test**, which opens the **Test** page. See [Test](/dashboard/test).

If your plan needs a subscription and you have none, the band says "Your agent is off for your users until you subscribe." Its link opens **Settings → Billing and usage**.

## Your tests

**Your tests** counts your test links and the assists tried with them: "Try the agent in your own app, in your browser only. Its assists and chats don't count toward your plan." While the agent is on, it has a **Go to Test** button. **See every test, step by step, on Test** opens the **Test** page.

## Who gets it

| Choice | Who has the agent |
| --- | --- |
| **Everyone** | Everyone who signs in to your app, and every tenant who signs up later. **See them in Audience** opens the list. |
| **Selected** | Only the tenants and people in your list. |
| **A percentage** | A share of your tenants or of your people, which you choose. |

A tenant is one of your own customers: the `tenantId` your app passes to `identify()`. If your app sends no tenants, the choices name people only.

**A percentage** is sticky. The same tenants or people are picked each time, so raising the number only adds more, and nobody loses the agent. Under it, **Who has it today** says about how many people that is. You can also keep a list beside a percentage: "Anyone in this list always has it."

### The list

For **Selected**, or alongside **A percentage**:

- **Add a tenant or person** searches your tenants and people. **Add by id** adds an id nobody has used yet: "The tenantId your app passes to identify()".
- **Add a list** pastes many at once: tenant ids or names, or emails or user ids. "Nothing changes until you press Save on the page."
- Past eight entries, **Find in the list** searches it. **Remove all** empties it.
- Each entry has its own **What it does** menu and a remove button.

## What it does

| Choice | What the agent does in an assist |
| --- | --- |
| **Does the steps** | It fills in the form and presses the buttons for them. |
| **Shows how** | It points at each field and waits while they do it. |

This is the choice for everyone. Anyone in your list can have their own: pick it from the menu on their row.

Either way, the agent hands some steps back to the user, such as uploads, codes and passwords. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).

## Assists and Chat messages

One panel for each. Each shows this month's use against your plan, with **Get more** at the end of the bar when packs are on sale.

Under it, **The most each can start a day** (assists) or **The most each can send a day** (chat messages) sets a daily limit for:

- **Each person**
- **Each tenant** (shown when your app sends tenants)
- **Your organisation**

"Leave it empty to use our default": an empty box shows that default in gray, or **No limit** when there is none. A box can't go above the most your plan allows; the page says so if you try. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage) for what your users see at a limit.

## How far over your plan

A slider from 0% up to the most your plan allows. It sets how far past your plan's assists and chat messages your users may go this month, billed as overage. It starts at the most your plan allows.

- Above 0%, it says how many more assists and messages that allows until your plan period starts again.
- At **0%**, everything stops at your plan's limit: "Stops at your plan's limit until 1 Nov." and "Nothing more is billed."
- During the free trial there is no overage. When the trial's assists or chat messages are used, the page says so, "until your plan starts on" the date.
- While a payment is failing, it says "Overages are paused until your last payment goes through." See [A failed payment](/dashboard/billing#a-failed-payment).
- **Buy extra on Billing** opens **Settings → Billing and usage** to buy a pack instead.

## Who used it

Assist and chat numbers for **This period**, **Last 7 days** or **Last 30 days**: **Started**, **Finished** and **People helped** for assists, **Conversations**, **Messages counted** and **Pages asked from** for chat. Click **Started** or **Finished** to open the assists behind it. **More in Reports** opens **Reports**.

At the foot of the page, **How we count** says: "An assist counts once HelpAlive completes its first step. Offers nobody started, and assists that stop or fail before their first step is done, are not counted. A chat message counts when the agent answers one. Tests never count."

## Check it works

1. Save with the agent on. The band reads "On for ...".
2. In your app, sign in as someone who should have it, and load a few pages.
3. Open the chat button and ask a question.

## Troubleshooting

- **The switch won't move to On.** No source is published. Publish one on **Train**.
- **The band says "On, but for nobody yet".** **Selected** is chosen with an empty list. Add tenants or people, or choose **Everyone**.
- **You turned it on and see no chat button.** Wait a minute and load a few pages. Check that `identify()` runs, and that **Who gets it** includes you.
- **"That limit can be set up to ..."** A daily limit is above what your plan allows. Lower the highlighted number.
- **Your users stopped getting assists.** You may have reached your plan's assists with **How far over your plan** at 0%, or your trial's assists. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage).
