This page explains what counts against your plan, the limits you can set, and what happens when one is reached. It is for admins who watch usage and billing. You set everything here on **Controls**, and you buy more on **Settings → Billing and usage**.

## How we count

> A guide counts once, when someone starts it; a guide only offered does not. A chat message counts when the agent answers one. The Test page never counts.

- A failed or empty answer does not count.
- The greeting and suggested questions the chat shows when it opens do not count.

Your plan includes a number of guides and chat messages each month. **Settings → Billing and usage** shows how many, and their prices.

## Your plan period

Usage runs by plan period, one month long. **Controls** shows the dates, for example "1 to 30 September", and each panel says the day it starts again.

## Daily limits per person, tenant and organisation

On **Controls**, the **Guides** and **Chat messages** panels each have a daily limit for:

| Limit | What it bounds |
| --- | --- |
| **Each person** | Guides one user can start, or chat messages one user can send, in a day. |
| **Each tenant** | The same for all the people in one of your tenants together. Shown when your app sends tenants. |
| **Your organisation** | The same for all your users together. |

Leave a box empty to use our default; the box shows it in grey. A box can't go above the most your plan allows. These limits protect your package from one heavy user or tenant. They are not your monthly allowance.

## How far over your package

**How far over your package** on **Controls** sets how far past your plan's monthly amounts your users may go. It is a percent of your package.

- **It starts at the most your plan allows.** Your users can go that far past your package, billed as overage at the end of the month. The page says how many more that allows.
- **0% means stop.** At your package, new guides and chat messages are refused until the period starts again.
- For more than the slider allows, buy a pack.
- **During the free trial there is no overage.** At the trial's guides or chat messages, new ones stop until your plan starts. **Controls** says your trial's guides or chat messages are used, "until your plan starts on" the date.
- **While a payment is failing**, overage pauses. See [A failed payment](/dashboard/billing#a-failed-payment).

Packs add to what your plan includes. They are used after the plan's own amount runs out, and they carry over while you stay subscribed. See [Billing and usage](/dashboard/billing#packs).

## What your users see at a limit

Your users never see a limit named.

- **At your plan's guides**, with nothing more allowed, the chat stops offering guides and keeps answering in words. A guide that starts anyway ends quietly, and the chat says "Let's pick this up in a moment."
- **At your plan's chat messages**, with nothing more allowed, a chat that is already open answers each new message with "Chat isn't available right now, please try again later." From the next page load the chat button is hidden. It comes back with a pack, a higher **How far over your package**, or the new plan period. Every guide starts from a chat message, so no new guides can start either until chat is available again.
- **At a daily limit** for a person, tenant or your organisation, the same quiet answers apply until the day is over.


## What you see

- **Controls** and **Home** show each kind against your plan. The bar turns amber at 80%.
- At your plan's amount, the **Controls** band says so, and whether usage goes on as overage.
- When something stops, the band says why and until when, with "Buy extra on the Billing page to add more now."
- **Home** lists a kind at 80% or more under **Needs you**, with **Add guides** or **Add chat messages**.

## Usage emails

We email everyone on your team with a confirmed email address:

- when guides or chat messages reach 80% of your plan;
- when your plan's amount is used up;
- when new ones are paused, for example because your last payment did not go through.

Each of these goes once per kind in a plan period. There is no setting to turn them off. **Your account** shows whether you get them.

## Check it works

Open **Controls**. Each panel shows a bar with this month's use. **How far over your package** shows your current percent.

## Troubleshooting

- **The chat stopped offering guides.** You have reached your plan's guides. Raise **How far over your package**, or buy a pack on **Settings → Billing and usage**.
- **The chat button is gone for everyone.** Your chat messages are used up, or a failed payment has paused the agent. Check **Controls** and **Settings → Billing and usage**.
- **One user says the agent stopped answering.** They may have reached **Each person**'s daily limit. Raise it on **Controls**, or wait for the next day.
- **The numbers look low.** Offers that were never started, and Test page runs, do not count.
