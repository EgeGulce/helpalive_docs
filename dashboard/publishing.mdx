This page explains which changes wait for **Publish** and which apply as soon as you save. It is for anyone on your team who edits the agent.

## What waits for Publish

These changes are drafts. Your users don't see them until you publish. The **Test** page reads them, so you can try them first.

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Train** | New websites and files, and a re-crawl you started yourself |
| **Train** | **Tone of voice** and **Answer length** |
| **Train** | Custom responses |
| **Appearance** | Every setting |

## What applies at once

These are never drafts. They apply when you save.

- **Controls**: the switch, **Who gets it**, **What it does**, the limits for each person, tenant and your organisation, and **How far over your package**. After **Save changes**, the toast says "Saved. It applies now." with **Undo**.
- **Train**: removing a source, and **Reset training data**.
- **Train**: an automatic sync goes live by itself, unless it is held for your check.
- **Settings**: everything on its four tabs.

## Publish your changes

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the Publish bar">
    While anything waits, a bar is pinned to the top of every dashboard page: **Unpublished changes**. Labels name what waits, such as **Appearance**, **Guidance** (tone and length), **Responses**, **New doc** or **Re-crawl**, with when you last changed it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview it">
    **Preview on Test** opens the **Test** page, which runs your questions against the draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Press **Publish**. The **Publish changes** dialog lists what goes live: **Config sections**, **Knowledge docs** and **New crawls**. For a new crawl it shows **Live now** against **New crawl**, so you can catch a crawl that found too few pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press **Publish now**. The toast says "Published. Live on your site now."
  </Step>
</Steps>

A new source that is still crawling stays a draft. While new sources are the only change and all still crawl, the bar says "Publish appears when the crawl finishes."

## Discard a draft

In the bar's menu, **More draft actions → Discard draft** opens **Discard unpublished changes?**. Press **Discard changes** to throw away every draft. A waiting re-crawl is thrown away too, and that source keeps what is live.

## Check it works

After you publish, the bar disappears. In your app, the widget picks up the change within a minute or a few page loads. Then ask the agent a question your change affects.

## Troubleshooting

- **Publish is greyed out and says "Nothing is ready yet."** Your new docs are still crawling. Publish once a crawl finishes.
- **A re-crawl "could not go live".** Re-crawl that source on **Train**, then publish again.
- **You changed Controls and see no Publish bar.** That is expected. Controls apply as soon as you save.
