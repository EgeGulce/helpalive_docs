**Home** is the first page of the dashboard and the first item in the sidebar. It tells you in one screen whether the agent is on, what needs your attention, and how much of your plan you have used.

## What is on the page

### Your agent

The band at the top says the agent's state in one sentence, with one button.

| State | What it says | Button |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No source published | **Off: no docs published.** It can turn on once one source is published on Train. | **Add them on Train** |
| Switched off | **Off, by your switch.** It says how many people would get it, or "Nobody sees it; your analytics keep running." | **Turn on** (opens Controls) |
| On | **On for everyone**, **On for 25% of tenants**, or **On for** the tenants and people in your list. It adds "showing how" when the agent shows the steps rather than doing them. | **Change who gets it** (opens Controls) |
| Paused for a failed payment | **Paused.** "Your last payment did not go through, so your users don't see it until your card is updated." | **Update card** (opens **Settings → Billing and usage**) |

While the agent is on, the second line says how many sources it answers from and when you last published.

### Needs you

Under the band, **Needs you** lists anything that waits for a person, each with a link. You can **Hide** or **Show** the list. It can hold:

- a Setup step not done yet, with **Open step 1** to **Open step 4**;
- a source whose crawl or re-crawl failed, with **See on Train**;
- a finished re-crawl that waits for **Publish** in the bar at the top;
- assists or chat messages at 80% or more of your plan, or in the last 7 days of your plan period. **Add assists** or **Add chat messages** shows when packs are on sale;
- an invite that ends within 3 days and has not been taken, with **Resend invite**;
- a card on file that expires within 30 days or has expired, with **Update card**.

### This month against your plan

Your plan's name and period, and for assists and chat messages: how many are used, how many your plan includes, and a projection once there is enough of the month to go on. Use **Add assists** or **Add chat messages** to buy a pack on **Settings → Billing and usage**.

### Last 7 days: What the agent did

Five figures for the last 7 days, each compared with the 7 days before:

- **People helped**: people who started at least one assist.
- **Assists started**
- **Finished**: the share of assists started that the agent took to the end.
- **Conversations**
- **Messages**: chat messages, as your plan counts them.

**Open in Reports** opens the same figures in **Reports**, where you can pick another period.

### Assists per day and how they ended

A bar a day for the last 7 days, split by how each assist ended. Before anyone has started an assist, it says "Assists show here once the agent is on and people start them."

### Latest activity

Assists and chats across your tenants, newest first, with a link into **Audience**.

## Check it works

Once Setup is done and the agent is on, the band reads "On for ..." and **Needs you** has no Setup lines. The figures fill in as people use the agent in your app.

## Troubleshooting

- **The band says "Off: no docs published." although you added sources.** A source counts only once it is published. Press **Publish** in the bar at the top.
- **The figures stay at zero.** Assists count once HelpAlive completes their first step, and chat messages when the agent answers. Tests never count. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage).
