**Privacy Management** is where you act on privacy requests from your users and choose how long their chat and assist text is kept. It is the fourth tab of **Settings**, at **Settings → Privacy Management**. It has four parts: **Chat and assist text**, **Tenants**, **People** and **History**.

## Chat and assist text

How long we keep the words of your users' chats and assists after they go quiet. After that the words are cleared; the counts, dates and outcomes stay.

The card also says: "We may use them, de-identified, to improve HelpAlive for all customers; write to us to opt out." To opt out, write to support@helpalive.com.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a time">
    Under **Keep for**, choose **30 days**, **90 days**, **180 days**, **1 year** (the default) or **2 years**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Press **Save**. A shorter time asks first: text older than it is cleared at the next daily clean-up and cannot be brought back. Press **Shorten it** to go ahead.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Assist step names and the labels clicked during an assist are not cleared by this setting. They stay until you delete that person's data.

## Tenants and People

**Tenants** lists each of your tenants, and **People** each signed-in user, with their **Tracking** state, **Why** it changed, **Changed by** and when. Search by tenant, user or email, and filter by **Status** (**Active** or **Suppressed**) and **Scope**. **Advanced filters** narrow by tenant id, user id, action, who acted and dates.

Acting on a tenant reaches all of its people.

### Stop tracking

**Stop tracking** stops recording new activity for them. What was recorded stays, and stays visible. For a tenant it asks first: **Stop tracking**, or **Keep tracking** to go back.

### Resume tracking

**Resume tracking** starts recording again for someone you stopped.

### Delete their data

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Delete their data">
    On the tenant's or person's row, press **Delete their data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a reason">
    Fill in **Why (required, kept in the history)**, for example "their admin asked us to".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Press **Delete for good**, or **Keep it** to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything recorded about them is deleted for good, including their chats and assists; for a tenant, its people and their visits too. Counts stay, without anyone attached. Tracking stays stopped until you resume it. The row shows **Deleting their data**, then **Data deleted, tracking stopped**.

<Warning>
Deleting cannot be undone. It does not reach copies in our server logs. They hold chat text and the page content an assist read, and age out after 30 days.
</Warning>

### Act on several at once

Tick rows, or the box that selects every row on the page, and choose **Stop Tracking**, **Resume** or **Delete** in the toolbar. Add a reason: optional for stop and resume, required for delete. **Validate only (no writes)** checks the action without running it. Press **Confirm**, or **Confirm delete**.

You can also act on one tenant or person from their drawer in **Audience**, under **Privacy**.

## History

**History** lists every privacy action across tenants and people, newest first: the **Time**, **Type**, **Action**, **Target**, **Actor** and **Reason**. Filter it by **Actor email**, **Action** and date.

## A copy of someone's data

To get a copy of a person's data, write to support@helpalive.com.

## Check it works

After you stop tracking someone, their row shows **Tracking stopped**, and **History** has a **Tracking stopped** line with your email.

## Troubleshooting

- **The row says "Deletion did not finish".** Run **Delete their data** again. If it keeps failing, write to support@helpalive.com.
- **A batch action is greyed out.** Stop works only when every selected row is active, and Resume only when every selected row is stopped.
- **You are asked for your password when saving the retention time.** That is expected. See [Your account](/dashboard/account#confirm-your-password).
