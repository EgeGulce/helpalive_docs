**Test** lets you try the agent before your users do. It is the third item in the sidebar, with two views, switched at the top right. **On your app**, the one it opens on, tries the whole agent in your real app; see [Test it on your app](#test-it-on-your-app). **Question batches** runs a batch of questions through the agent so you can read its answers. Use it after you change sources, tone, length or custom responses on **Train**, before you press **Publish**.

## What it tests

- The page runs your questions through the same answering steps as the chat in your app.
- While you have unpublished changes, **Question batches** uses them, and the page shows **Previewing draft**. The input card says "Unpublished changes will be included."
- With nothing unpublished, it runs against what is live, "the same setup end users get on your site."
- Test runs never count against your plan.

Question batches test chat answers only. To try assists too, use [Test it on your app](#test-it-on-your-app). Nothing here reaches your users until you **Publish**.

## Run a test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new test">
    Press **New test**. Give it a **Test name (optional)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add questions">
    Type one question per line under **Questions (one per line)**. To test follow-ups, press **Add conversation** and add an **Opening question** with up to four **Follow-up** questions. A run holds up to 50 questions and follow-ups in all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or load questions">
    **Load set** opens a set you saved before. **Upload CSV** reads a file with columns `turn_1`, `turn_2` and so on, one conversation per row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run it">
    Press **Run test**. Answers stream into the results table as each question finishes. **Cancel** stops the run after the current question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the results

Each run appears in the list on the left with its state: **Queued**, **Running**, **Done**, **Cancelled** or **Error**. Open a run to see each question and the agent's answer. Click a row for **Question detail**, including the sections of your docs the answer drew on.

The summary strip at the top says how the run went, for example "Finished all 12 questions". From there you can:

- save the questions as a reusable set, to run them again later;
- export the results as a CSV file;
- **Compare** it with another run.

## Compare two runs

Open a finished run and press **Compare** in its summary strip. The **Compare runs** view opens. Pick a second run under **Pick a run to compare…**. HelpAlive pairs the answers by question and marks each pair **Identical**, **Different**, **Only in A** or **Only in B**. Run the same set before and after a change on **Train** to see what the change did.

## Test it on your app

**Test → On your app** lets you try the whole agent, assists included, in your real app, before you turn it on for anyone. Step 3 of **Settings → Setup & API Key** brings you here.

You don't need to change **Controls** or pick yourself there. **Test it on your app** opens your app with the agent on in your browser only, even while it is off for your users. Nobody else sees it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your app">
    Our script and `identify()` have to be on your app first. Enter your app's address and press **Check this address**. The check passes once we have seen our script run there and a signed-in person use it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a test link">
    Sign in to your app as the person you'll test with, then press **Test it on your app**. Your app opens in a new tab with the link. The agent shows in that browser only, with a **Test** badge, even while it is off for everyone else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **How testing works** box sums up the rules below: one link per browser, links last 14 days, and tests don't count toward your plan.

**One link, one browser.** The first browser that opens a link keeps it for 14 days. The link is shown once and we keep no copy, so it does nothing in another browser, a private window or on another computer. To test somewhere else, make a new link. A link nobody has opened yet can still be copied from the browser that made it.

**Revoke** stops a link: the browser holding it stops seeing the agent on its next page. **Stop testing** on the widget's Test strip does the same from inside your app. Your users see the agent only once they are in **Who gets it** on [Controls](/dashboard/controls); a test link never changes that.

Tests don't count toward your plan or your users' daily limits. Your workspace gets a monthly allowance of test assists and test chats, counted as tests start, shown at the top of **On your app** as **Test assists** and **Test chats**. Every assist and chat from a test link appears there, step by step. The step on **Setup** turns green once a test has run an assist or a chat.

## Check it works

Run one question your docs answer. The row turns **Done** and shows an answer drawn from your sources.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Run test" is disabled.** The run is over the 50-turn cap. Remove some questions or a conversation.
- **A CSV row was skipped.** Rows with more than five turns are skipped. Rows past the 50-turn cap are dropped.
- **The test link does nothing in this browser.** Each link works in the first browser that opens it. Make a new link for this browser.
- **The link was lost while signing in.** If your app signs people in on another address, sign in, then open the link once more.
- **The answer ignores a source you just added.** Check that it finished processing on **Train**. A source still processing is not part of the draft yet.
