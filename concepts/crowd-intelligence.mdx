Crowd intelligence is the engine at the core of HelpAlive. Instead of asking you to define what "good" looks like, HelpAlive observes what your users actually do — across thousands of sessions — and extracts the common patterns that lead to successful outcomes. The result is a living behavioral map of your product: a model of exactly what successful users do at every step, automatically derived from real usage.

## How the behavioral map is built

Every time a user completes a key action in your product — finishing onboarding, publishing a record, connecting an integration — HelpAlive records the sequence of interactions that led there: which pages they visited, which elements they clicked, in what order, and at what pace. Across many sessions, consistent patterns emerge. HelpAlive aggregates those patterns into a behavioral map that represents the high-probability path to success at each point in your product.

You do not define these paths. You do not tag events or configure funnels. HelpAlive derives the map entirely from observed behavior. The more sessions it sees, the more precise the map becomes.

## What deviation means

A deviation occurs when a user's live session begins to diverge from the behavioral map. This might look like:

- Spending significantly longer on a step than successful users typically do
- Clicking an element that successful users rarely touch at that stage
- Backtracking to a page they already visited
- Stalling entirely after a particular interaction

HelpAlive does not rely on a single signal. It weighs the combination of signals against the behavioral map to determine whether a user is genuinely off-track or simply taking a slightly different-but-valid route. Only a meaningful divergence triggers an intervention.

## Why this is better than rule-based triggers

Rule-based guidance systems require someone to anticipate every failure mode in advance and write a condition for it. That means the rules are always behind the product: they miss failure patterns you did not predict, they go stale when the product changes, and they require ongoing maintenance.

HelpAlive does not need you to predict where users will get stuck. The behavioral map continuously learns from real sessions, so it adapts as your product evolves and as user behavior shifts. New failure patterns are detected automatically — no rule updates, no re-tagging, no maintenance cycle.

| Approach | Setup required | Adapts to product changes | Catches unpredicted failures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule-based triggers | Manual event tagging + condition authoring | No — must be updated manually | No |
| HelpAlive crowd intelligence | None | Yes — learns from new sessions | Yes |

<Tip>
  Accuracy improves with session volume. HelpAlive starts forming a behavioral map from your first sessions, but guidance becomes progressively more precise as the map is reinforced by more data. Products with higher traffic see confident detection faster. For lower-traffic products, the map stabilizes over days rather than hours — and it continues to refine itself indefinitely as long as sessions are flowing.
</Tip>
