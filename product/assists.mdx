An assist is HelpAlive doing a task in your app for a user, or showing them how, one step at a time. This page shows what the user sees from the offer to the recap, in the widget's own words. Inside the widget, your users see an assist as a "walkthrough"; the dashboard calls it an assist.

## Prerequisites

- The agent is on for the user on **Controls**, and you have published at least one source on **Train**.
- On **Controls**, you have chosen **Does the steps** or **Shows how** for this user, their tenant, or everyone.

Every assist starts from the chat. Each one is put together for the user's request and the page in front of them.

## Does the steps, or shows how

| Mode on **Controls** | Offer button | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Does the steps** | **Let's do it** | HelpAlive clicks, types, picks and scrolls for the user, including Save, Submit and Delete. It hands back the steps only the user can do. |
| **Shows how** | **Show me how** | HelpAlive highlights each control and says what to do. The user does every click. |

## Starting an assist

1. The user asks for something in the chat, such as "Can you add a teammate for me?"
2. An offer card appears under the answer with the task's name and **My plan**, the rough steps.
3. The user presses **Let's do it** or **Show me how**. The button reads "Getting it ready…" while the walkthrough gets ready.

The card then shows **In progress** while the walkthrough runs.

## The bar

A bar sits at the bottom of the page for the whole walkthrough. On its left, a line says who is acting:

- **HelpAlive:** followed by what it's doing, such as working out the next step or waiting for the page.
- **Your turn:** followed by what the user should do. A **Your turn** tag also shows.

The current part of the task shows above that line.

When it's the user's turn, the control is highlighted on the page with a short instruction. If there's text to type, it shows with a copy button. **Done** tells HelpAlive the user did the step. **Skip** moves past it.

When HelpAlive needs something only the user knows, such as a name or a message's wording, it asks in a small card. The user picks or types an answer, or presses **Skip**.

On the right of the bar are four controls.

### Flightpath

**Flightpath** opens the walkthrough's plan and a live record of what has happened. It shows each step, who did it, and any questions and answers.

### Ask or steer

**Ask or steer** opens a box that says "Ask anything, or say what to change…". The user types and presses **Send**. HelpAlive waits while it reads the words, then does one of two things:

- It answers a question in the chat.
- It changes what it does next, such as "use the Marketing team instead". The walkthrough carries on with the change.

### Take over and Hand back

With **Does the steps**, the bar shows **Take over**. Pressing it hands every remaining step to the user, with HelpAlive showing the way. **Hand back** lets HelpAlive do the steps again from the next one. With **Shows how**, these buttons don't appear, because every step is already the user's.

### Pause and Resume

**Pause** stops the walkthrough where it is and opens the chat. The chat shows the walkthrough's name with **Cancel** and **Resume**. **Resume** carries on from the same step, and **Cancel** ends the walkthrough.

If the user steps away, the walkthrough waits, and the bar says "Paused. I'll pick this up when you're back." If the page reloads mid-walkthrough, it picks up where it left off.

## Checks along the way

After each step, HelpAlive reads the page again to check the step landed. If a press didn't land, it hands that step to the user rather than pressing again. Before deleting records chosen by a rule, it names them and asks once. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).

## The recap

When the walkthrough ends, a recap card appears in the chat:

- **Completed** or **Stopped** at the top. A stopped walkthrough says why, such as "You stopped this walkthrough.", and where it stopped.
- **You did**, the steps the user did, with any hand-overs and questions.
- **I did**, the steps HelpAlive did.
- **Left for you**, anything not finished.

The offer card then offers **Run again**, or **Continue from where we left off** when the walkthrough can carry on.

## What counts as an assist

**An assist counts once HelpAlive completes its first step.** It counts once, however it ends after that and however long it runs.

These don't count:

- An offer the user doesn't start.
- An assist that stops or fails before its first step is done.
- An assist that can't start because a limit was reached, or because chat messages are used up.
- Assists from a test link. See [Test it on your app](/dashboard/test#test-it-on-your-app).

**Run again** and **Continue from where we left off** each start a new assist. Pausing, **Take over**, **Hand back** and steering stay within the same one.

Every assist starts from a chat message. The agent's answer counts as one chat message whether or not the user starts the assist, and so does each question asked with **Ask or steer**.

On your dashboard, **Assists started** is the count your plan uses. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage).

## Check it works

1. On **Controls**, set **Does the steps** for yourself.
2. In your app, ask the chat to do a short task your docs describe.
3. Press **Let's do it** and watch the bar switch between **HelpAlive:** and **Your turn:**.
4. Press **Take over**, then **Hand back**, then **Pause** and **Resume**.
5. Let it finish and read the recap's **You did** and **I did**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The button says Show me how, not Let's do it.** This user, or their tenant, is set to **Shows how** on **Controls**. Changes there apply at once.
- **No Take over button.** It appears only with **Does the steps**, and not while the walkthrough is paused.
- **No offer at all.** The agent offers only tasks it can do in your app. Offers also stop once your assists for the month are used up. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage).
- **The assist stops at a step on another site or in a file dialog.** That's a hand-back by design. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).
