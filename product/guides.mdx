A guide is HelpAlive doing a task in your app for a user, or showing them how, one step at a time. This page shows what the user sees from the offer to the recap, in the widget's own words. Inside the widget, users also see a guide called a "walkthrough".

## Prerequisites

- The agent is on for the user on **Controls**, and you have published at least one source on **Train**.
- On **Controls**, you have chosen **Does the steps** or **Shows how** for this user, their tenant, or everyone.

Every guide starts from the chat. Each one is put together for the user's request and the page in front of them.

## Does the steps, or shows how

| Mode on **Controls** | Offer button | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Does the steps** | **Let's do it** | HelpAlive clicks, types, picks and scrolls for the user, including Save, Submit and Delete. It hands back the steps only the user can do. |
| **Shows how** | **Guide me** | HelpAlive highlights each control and says what to do. The user does every click. |

## Starting a guide

1. The user asks for something in the chat, such as "Can you add a teammate for me?"
2. An offer card appears under the answer with the task's name and **My plan**, the rough steps.
3. The user presses **Let's do it** or **Guide me**. The button reads "Preparing your guide…" while it gets ready.

The card then shows **In progress** while the guide runs.

## The bar

A bar sits at the bottom of the page for the whole guide. On its left, a line says who is acting:

- **HelpAlive:** followed by what it's doing, such as working out the next step or waiting for the page.
- **Your turn:** followed by what the user should do. A **Your turn** tag also shows.

The current part of the task shows above that line.

When it's the user's turn, the control is highlighted on the page with a short instruction. If there's text to type, it shows with a copy button. **Done** tells HelpAlive the user did the step. **Skip** moves past it.

When HelpAlive needs something only the user knows, such as a name or a message's wording, it asks in a small card. The user picks or types an answer, or presses **Skip**.

On the right of the bar are four controls.

### Flightpath

**Flightpath** opens the guide's plan and a live record of what has happened. It shows each step, who did it, and any questions and answers.

### Ask or steer

**Ask or steer** opens a box that says "Ask anything, or say what to change…". The user types, then presses one of two buttons:

- **Ask** pauses the guide and answers the question in the chat.
- **Steer** changes what HelpAlive does next, such as "use the Marketing team instead". The guide carries on with the change.

### Take over and Hand back

With **Does the steps**, the bar shows **Take over**. Pressing it hands every remaining step to the user, with HelpAlive showing the way. **Hand back** lets HelpAlive do the steps again from the next one. With **Shows how**, these buttons don't appear, because every step is already the user's.

### Pause and Resume

**Pause** stops the guide where it is and opens the chat. The chat shows the guide's name with **Cancel** and **Resume**. **Resume** carries on from the same step, and **Cancel** ends the guide.

If the user steps away, the guide waits, and the bar says it will pick up when they're back. If the page reloads mid-guide, it picks up where it left off.

## Checks along the way

After each step, HelpAlive reads the page again to check the step landed. If a press didn't land, it hands that step to the user rather than pressing again. Before deleting records chosen by a rule, it names them and asks once. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).

## The recap

When the guide ends, a recap card appears in the chat:

- **Completed** or **Stopped** at the top. A stopped guide says why, such as "You stopped this walkthrough.", and where it stopped.
- **You did**, the steps the user did, with any hand-overs and questions.
- **I did**, the steps HelpAlive did.
- **Left for you**, anything not finished.

The offer card then offers **Run again**, or **Continue from where we left off** when the guide can carry on.

## Check it works

1. On **Controls**, set **Does the steps** for yourself.
2. In your app, ask the chat to do a short task your docs describe.
3. Press **Let's do it** and watch the bar switch between **HelpAlive:** and **Your turn:**.
4. Press **Take over**, then **Hand back**, then **Pause** and **Resume**.
5. Let it finish and read the recap's **You did** and **I did**.

## Troubleshooting

- **The button says Guide me, not Let's do it.** This user, or their tenant, is set to **Shows how** on **Controls**. Changes there apply at once.
- **No Take over button.** It appears only with **Does the steps**, and not while the guide is paused.
- **No offer at all.** The agent offers only tasks it can do in your app. Offers also stop once your guides for the month are used up. See [Limits and usage](/dashboard/usage).
- **The guide stops at a step on another site or in a file dialog.** That's a hand-back by design. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).
