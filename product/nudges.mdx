A nudge is a short note from HelpAlive that offers help when a user gets stuck in your app. It appears beside the chat launcher at the moment of trouble, so users get help without having to think of asking.

## Nudges at a glance

- A nudge appears when a user gets stuck: a save your app refuses, an error on the page, or a button that doesn't work for them.
- It names the problem in your app's own words, such as the error your page shows.
- One click opens the chat with the user's question already written.
- Ignoring or closing a nudge does nothing. It never saves or sends anything for the user.
- Nudges are on for every workspace. You can turn them off on **Controls**.

## What your users see

<Steps>
  <Step title="A short note beside the launcher">
    The note names what went wrong, for example `That didn't save: "This URL already exists". Want me to help you fix it?` After a few seconds it folds into a dot on the launcher, and it comes back while the user's pointer is over the launcher. The dot stays when the user moves to another page or reloads, in the same tab, until 30 minutes after it folded into the dot. A new nudge replaces it. A nudge the user closed or clicked doesn't come back.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Their question, already written">
    Clicking the note opens the chat with a question in the box, for example `I'm getting "This URL already exists" when I save. How do I fix it?` The user can send it, change it or type their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An answer, and an assist when it helps">
    The agent answers as it does any question, using the page the user is on. When it can fix the problem in your app, it offers an assist. See [Assists](/product/assists).
  </Step>
</Steps>

A nudge quotes only words your app shows on the page, never words that look personal, such as an email address. Users never see the same line twice in a row.

## Turn nudges on or off

Nudges reach the same people as the chat. To turn them off for everyone, use the **Nudges** switch on **Controls**. See [Controls](/dashboard/controls).

## See what nudges led to

Each nudge appears in **Latest activity** with what came of it. A chat or assist that started from a nudge carries a card saying why the nudge appeared. See [Home](/dashboard/home).
