HelpAlive is an in-app AI agent: it answers your users' questions from your docs, and does the task for them in your app or shows them how. This page explains where HelpAlive fits next to the other ways SaaS teams help users inside their product. It is for teams choosing a tool, and for anyone asking what an in-app AI agent is.

## HelpAlive compared at a glance

- A help chatbot stops at the answer. HelpAlive also reads the page the user is on and offers to do the task or show them how.
- Product tours are written in advance and break when your interface changes. HelpAlive writes no tours ahead of time.
- HelpAlive needs no API or MCP server. It works through your app's own interface, in the user's own signed-in session.
- HelpAlive takes no screenshots. It reads the page as structured text.
- HelpAlive runs in web apps in the browser, for signed-in users that your app identifies.

## What is an in-app AI agent?

An in-app AI agent is an assistant that runs inside your web app, sees the page your user is on, and can act there. HelpAlive is one: it answers your users' questions from your docs, and does the task for them in your app or shows them how, one step at a time. You add it with one script tag and one `identify()` call.

## How is HelpAlive different from a help chatbot?

A help chatbot answers questions from your help center. HelpAlive answers from your docs too, but it also reads the page the user is on, which means that it can answer about what they see. When the user wants something done, it offers to do it in your app or show them how. A chatbot stops at the answer.

## How is it different from product tours?

Product tours are written by your team in advance, step by step, and break when your interface changes. HelpAlive writes no tours ahead of time. Each assist starts from what a user asks in the chat and follows your app as it is at that moment. There is nothing to author or tag.

## Do I need an API or an MCP server?

No. Agents built on an API or MCP server can only do what that API exposes, which is why your team has to build and maintain it first. HelpAlive works through your app's own interface, in the user's own signed-in session. It can do only what that user could do themselves, and it hands back steps only the user should take, such as typing a password or uploading a file. See [What the agent won't do](/product/what-it-wont-do).

## Does HelpAlive take screenshots?

No. Agents that work from screenshots send images of the screen to a model and wait for it to read them at every step. HelpAlive reads the page as structured text: headings, visible text, and each button, link and field with its label and state. Nothing is recorded as an image. Each step needs less work, which is why assists respond faster. See [How the AI uses your data](/trust/ai-and-your-data).

## Does it slow down my app?

No. The script doesn't hold up your page, because it loads in the background. The chat loads only after `identify()`, and the code for assists loads only when one starts. See [Install the script](/installation).

## When another tool fits better

- **You need human support agents, tickets or an inbox.** HelpAlive has no handoff to a human team. Keep your help desk for that.
- **Your product is a native mobile or desktop app.** HelpAlive runs in web apps in the browser.
- **You want users to stay anonymous.** The agent appears only for signed-in users that your app identifies.
