This page defines the words HelpAlive uses in the dashboard, the widget and these docs. Read it once before you set up **Controls** or install the script.

## People

### Tenant

A tenant is one of your customers: a company, team or workspace that uses your app. You send its id as `tenantId` in `identify()`, and its name as `tenantName`. Users with no tenant, as in a B2C app, are left without `tenantId` and appear together in your dashboard as **B2C customers**.

Tenants appear on **Controls** ("Each tenant"), on **Audience**, and on **Settings → Privacy Management**. You can set the agent's mode and limits per tenant.

### User

A user is a person who uses your app. You tell HelpAlive who they are with `identify()`, using your own id for them as `userId`. The agent appears only for users you identify. See [Identify users](/sdk/identify).

### Your team

Your team is your own staff who sign in to the HelpAlive dashboard. Everyone on your team is an admin and can change every setting. You invite teammates on **Settings → Team & access**.

### Verified user

A verified user is one whose identity your server has signed with your signing secret. With **Only allow verified users** on, HelpAlive refuses chat, assist and usage requests that don't carry a valid signed token. See [Verify users](/sdk/verify-users).

## What the agent does

### Assist

An assist is the agent doing a task in your app for a user, or showing them how, one step at a time. It starts when a user accepts an offer in the chat. Inside the widget, your users see an assist as a "walkthrough". See [Assists](/product/assists).

An assist counts once HelpAlive completes its first step. An assist that was only offered, or stopped before then, doesn't count. See [What counts as an assist](/product/assists#what-counts-as-an-assist).

### Chat message

A chat message counts when the agent answers one of your users' messages. The **Test** page never counts.

## What the agent knows

### Source

A source is something the agent answers from. You add sources on **Train**:

- **Add your help or docs site** reads every page your help center or docs site links to.
- **Upload a file** takes a PDF, MD, TXT or DOCX file up to 10 MB.

### Draft and published

New sources, and changes to tone, length, custom responses and **Appearance**, start as drafts. Your users see only what you have published. The **Test** page uses your drafts, so you can try changes before you press **Publish**. Changes on **Controls** apply at once, with no draft. See [Publishing](/dashboard/publishing).

## Billing

### Plan

Your plan includes a number of assists and chat messages each month. **Settings → Billing and usage** shows that number as **In your plan** (**In your trial** during the free trial). On **Controls**, **How far over your plan** sets how far usage may go past it.

### Pack

A pack is extra assists or chat messages you buy on top of your plan, with **Add assists** or **Add chat messages**. Packs are charged to the card on file and added right away. They are used after your plan's amount, carry over month to month, and end with your subscription. See [Billing and usage](/dashboard/billing).

## Keys

### Project key

The project key identifies your workspace in the script tag (`data-api-key`). It is public: it sits in your pages, where anyone can see it. Find it on **Settings → Setup & API Key**.

### Signing secret

The signing secret is the private key your server uses to sign user tokens for verified users. It stays on your server, for example in `HELPALIVE_SIGNING_SECRET`, and never goes into your pages. Find and rotate it on **Settings → Setup & API Key**. See [Security](/trust/security).
